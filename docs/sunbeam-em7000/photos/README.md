# Photographs of the machine as found

Photographs of the reference machine, taken by its owner during the as-found inspection (October 2026). The owner releases each into the public domain, on the same terms as the rest of this repository. Each is reduced to about 1600 px on its long side and re-encoded as JPEG; the originals are the owner's.

Three of them are derived, not as taken: `original-board-component-side-aligned.jpg` is the original board's component side, squared up, and `original-board-solder-side-aligned.jpg` is its solder side, mirrored left to right and perspective-warped so that the images are aligned on the mounting holes and matched pads, areas of bare board and board flex not being constrained, in the component-side image's frame (1602 px wide, 12 px per mm, origin at the lower left, JPEG quality 70). The two were registered on the four mounting holes and 42 matched pads. The four holes agree to within 0.12 mm; the pads have a median disagreement of 0.25 mm and a maximum of 0.73 mm, over 46 scored points. These figures are the residuals of the one-off registration the owner ran; they are not reproducible from this repository, which holds the result and not the registration. Positions in the record are taken from the tables, not measured from these images; the image frame (origin at the lower left, 12 px per mm) is not tied to the datum to better than about 1 mm. `original-board-front-back-blend.jpg` blends the two aligned images 50/50, the component side and the registered, mirrored solder side, in natural colour, on the same pixel frame and canvas (12 px per mm, JPEG quality 55). The fit rests on pads visible on both sides: areas of bare board and the board's flex are not constrained, so a position read off the pair away from a pad carries more than the pad figures.

Each photograph supports the entries of the record that cite it by file name.
