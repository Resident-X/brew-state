### 1. Where the surge clamp at the cable connector sits, and which parts share its net (5.1, 7.27)

D402 (SMF6.0A, `USB_VBUS` pad 1, `GND` pad 2) stands at (87.21, 88.64), 3.99 mm from J402 pin 5 (87.49, 84.81), on the connector side of F401, as the plan's BOARD-1 asks. It hangs on a spur off J402 pin 5: 0.63 + 0.81 + 1.77 + 0.75 + 0.75 + 0.9 + 0.75 mm of 0.2 mm track, about 6.4 mm with one layer change (a via at (90.0, 87.25)); the path to F401 leaves J402 pin 5 on its own track (F.Cu then via (86.75, 82.75), B.Cu 10.25 mm, via (86.75, 72.5)) and does not pass D402. D402's `GND` pad is part of the F.Cu `GND` pour.

The net `/Controller/USB_VBUS` holds four pads only: J402 pin 5, D402 pad 1, D401 pad 5 (the USBLC6-2SC6's VBUS pin, 78.6, 91.45, reached by a B.Cu track and two vias) and F401 pad 1. The clamp therefore sits at the connector and shares its net with the data-line clamp and the fuse only. What the schematic review's finding is about lies behind F401: net `/USB_VBUS_F` (F401 pad 2, JP301 pad 3, TP413), which JP301 joins to `+5V` in the cable position, and the `+5V` net holds 37 parts (C201, C206, C303, C671, C676, C791, D205, D206, D731, D741, J520, J523 to J525, J570, J571, J630, J635, JP301, JP761, JP791, K201, K731, K741, Q822, R703, R713, R823, R824, R828, TP206, U201, U203, U302, U630, U631, U791). A fuse does not limit voltage, so the 10.3 V clamp level reaches all of them in the cable position; the clamp's position cannot change that.

Disposition: **the clamp sits at the connector as the finding's return condition requires** ("returns to the schematic if layout shows the clamp cannot sit at the connector": it can, and does). The parts on the net behind the fuse are as the schematic has them and stay under the owner's acceptance (b) (the cable position is a mains-off bench state) and (q). Layout carries no further change. One observation: the `USB_VBUS` tracks are 0.2 mm (the net is not in `Supply5V`); in the cable position the board's 5 V loads would run through them and through F401, 0.2 mm being 21 K of rise at 1 A by IPC-2221 external; USB cannot supply that, so the cable position cannot reach it. No finding.

### 2. How close the data-line clamp sits to the receptacle (5.2, 7.3, 7.38)

D401 pad 1 stands 4.90 mm from J402 pin 1 (the DP line) and pad 3 stands 4.90 mm from J402 pin 2 (the DM line), inside the 5 mm the plan holds it to (ST AN4879's "as close to the connector as possible"). The lines run through the clamp's pads, J402 to D401 first (J402 pin 1 to D401 pin 1: 3.14 + 1.6 mm of track on F.Cu; pin 2 to pin 3 through In3.Cu, 0.69 + 3.14 mm), then on to U401. D401's ground has a via 1.47 mm from its pad. Disposition: **met as the finding's ground requires** ("reads the clamp as close to the receptacle as the note requires"). The residue at PA11 and PA12 (17 V at 5 A against 6 V) is a part limit that no layout distance changes, and stays under the owner's acceptance (f) and (q).

### 3. Whether the service level the owner declares needs a clamp on the panel keys and knobs (5.18, 7.37, 7.39)

The owner's declared level is entry (v) of the residual-risk decision (2026-11-06): the board is kept as drawn, service reach is handled by procedure (the enclosure is opened to fit, test and replace parts, a service person discharges before touching a connector), the panel keys are read at bring-up, and clamps are added only if the owner later asks. Earlier records (`circuit-proof.md` F6) took IEC 61000-4-2 level 4 air as an assumed level for the operator controls; the owner's decision (v) is the operative one. On the finished board the nets `KEY1` to `KEY5`, `KNOB_HOT`, `KNOB_STEAM` and `TANK_LEVEL` carry exactly: the connector pin, a pull-up resistor, a test point, the controller pin, and for the knobs and tank a filter capacitor (C861, C862, C863); no clamp part and no unfitted clamp footprint is on any of them.

Disposition: **the declared level does not call for a clamp, and the layout carries none, consistent with the declaration.** If the owner later asks for clamps, the board has no footprint to populate and the change is a schematic and layout revision (the finding's own return condition). The routes are long (LR-04).

### 4. Whether the entry's filtering meets the emission limit where one is stated, and that none is stated where it is not (5.20, 5.22, 7.41, 7.43)

The entry's filtering is C101 alone, a 100 nF X2 line to neutral at (15.5, 69.7); the board carries no common-mode choke and no line-to-earth capacitor (no footprint carries an inductor or choke designator except the bead FB301 in the 3.3 V analogue supply), as the schematic judgement EMSRC-1 decided (`circuit-verdicts.md`, Mains entry row; DEL-HW-MAIN-BOARD-DESIGNED.C23). No emission limit is stated anywhere the board's records read: `open-items.md` 4.20 records "best effort, no emissions test planned", the CISPR 14.1 limit rows are not held, and `REQ-PRODUCTION-GRADE-VERIFICATION-001.C2` puts a measurement against "the governing standard's limits" at the production-grade stage, with no figure. **Disposition: none is stated; the layout therefore meets no limit and none is claimed.** The layout bears on the filter's effect by the distance of C101 from the entry (LR-01): the record's estimate of 19.5 to 49.9 dB is for an ideal capacitor and the finished lead of about 41 mm takes about 14 dB off it at 5 MHz.
