# Main board: is every signal conditioned to its requirement, and has anything changed since it was proven

A study of the main board's final design, board commit `c25f3af6`, from the repository's records and the routed board file, with the records brought to their state after the flow-input proof was re-run on the fitted buffer and the entry-fuse acceptance of 2026-11-05 was recorded.

## Provenance

Produced 2026-11-05 from board commit c25f3af6, by Claude agents; models, no physical measurement. No board has been built, so every figure here is datasheet arithmetic or a simulation of a model, and the comparisons of decks and scripts against the schematic were made by machine on that commit. The study's own arithmetic is marked as such. Line numbers cite the records as they stood at that commit; `circuit-proof.md` has since changed in its flow rows.

## 0. Bottom line (plain words)

- **No resistor, capacitor or part value on the board differs from what the proofs used.** I checked this by machine, not by trusting the records (section 2). The routed board (444 footprints, 1,108 of 1,109 pad nets) equals the schematic; the schematic equals `parts-specification.yaml` (444 of 444).
- **One MISMATCH was found, on the flow-meter input** (first below). It was a fit-state mismatch, not a value mismatch, and it is resolved: the generator writes the committed sheets, and the flow decks model the fitted buffer and were re-run (`circuit-proof.md`, the two flow rows).
- **The evidence is paper and simulation only.** No board has been built, so there is no measurement. Every "proven" below means "shown by datasheet arithmetic and SPICE under stated worst cases". Graph satisfaction verdicts for `SOL-HW-MAIN-BOARD-SCHEMATIC-PROVEN.C1`, `.C12` and `SOL-HW-MAIN-BOARD-PROVEN-AND-LAID-OUT.C1`, `.C7`, `.C8` read `unknown`. The final pre-order check FAILED (2,042 violations, `pre-order-check-result.md`) and layout review round 2 is not closed (25 pass, 62 fail, 88 findings, `layout-review.md` line 2316). Those are layout and mains-spacing items, not conditioning values, but the board is not "all green".
- **Measuring everything:** the temperature, pressure (digital), tank, knob, key, mains-presence, supply-voltage and supply-current paths are electrically conditioned correctly against the figures that exist. **Nine measurement paths cannot be proven because no requirement figure exists** (wand, brew pressure, analogue fallbacks, current inputs, zero-cross, supply voltage, supply current, in-stream probes, load cells; list in section 3).
- **Controlling everything:** the drive stages (heaters, pumps, valves, gauge, indicators) pass their electrical figures. What is open is thermal or surge data the makers do not publish, a coil inductance nobody has measured, and owner-accepted risks (a)-(m).

### Mismatches found, and their state

1. **Flow input fit state (R522, U520, C521; also R572, R576). Still stands as a tool limit, not a design fault.** The committed schematic symbols say `dnp no` and in-BOM for R522 (0 ohm link), R572 and R576 (value text "DNP"). The board footprints carry `(attr smd dnp)` and the records say R522, R572, R576 are not fitted and U520 and C521 are. A BOM cut from the schematic would fit the 0 ohm link R522 across U520 (FLOW_F to FLOW_IN) and short its input to its output (schematic-review finding 6.73, `pre-order-check-result.md`, `parts-specification.yaml`). The schematic editor's tools cannot set the flag; the fab BOM is cut from the board attributes (the exporter's exclude-do-not-populate option), and the board check lists the three expected parity lines (`SOL-HW-DO-NOT-POPULATE-PARITY-LINES-ARE-LISTED-AND-BOARD-DRIVES-BILL`).
2. **The flow proof ran a different configuration. Resolved.** `C_flow_pp.cir` and `C_flow_od.cir` modelled R522 as a 1 mohm link and no U520. Both decks now model the board's fit (U520 fitted with its input leakage of 5 uA, input capacitance and worst-case Schmitt thresholds, C521 fitted, R522 absent) and were re-run with ngspice; the two flow rows of `circuit-proof.md` carry the new results. No verdict changed: the filter node reaches 5.251 V against the buffer's 5.5 V recommended input (was 5.244 V at the pin with the link modelled), the open-drain high level is 3.242 V against the buffer's 2.74 V bound, the pin sees at most the 3.6 V rail, and the meter's low level is still not in any record. Two figures moved without changing a verdict: the low-level bound is the buffer's 0.89 V, 0.10 V tighter than the pin's own 0.3 x VDD at 3.3 V, and the open-drain edge reaches the buffer's threshold 0.83 ms after release (0.40 ms to the pin's VIH before).
3. **The proof generator would undo the fit. Resolved.** `circuit-proof/C_gen_sheets.py` wrote R522 `dnp: false` and U520 `dnp: true` and the old `TestPoint_Pad_D1.5mm` footprint. It now writes sheets 50, 52 and 57 byte for byte as committed, `C_check_sheets.py` reports any difference between its output and the committed sheets, and a test (`hardware/tools/tests/test_circuit_proof_sheet_generator.py`) runs both in a copy.

## 2. Has it changed since it was proven (done mechanically)

**What is the proof generated from?** Only part of it. `C_gen_sheets.py` is a parts table that writes sheets 50, 52, 57 and the C decks (C_front_ends.py reads it), so C values cannot drift from that table. The A, D, E and b scripts and decks carry **hand-copied constants** (`A_gen_decks.py`, `D_margins.py`, `E_*.py`, `b_*.py` read no schematic or sheet file). `circuit-proof.md` line 8 says the schematic would be exported to a SPICE netlist and each deck "re-run against it and compared with the committed netlist before placement". **No such export is committed** (no netlist file in `circuit-proof/`); the sentence was stale ("There is no KiCad schematic yet") and has since been replaced by one that says what the decks are written from. The comparison below stands in for an export.

**Baselines.** Proof decks and scripts were last committed `0b59ece6` (2026-10-31), with test-point removals in `5231707b`. Rows in `circuit-proof.md` last changed `561ea01d` (2026-11-05). Schematic review round 6 judged commit `502f317e`/`e16d375b`. I exported KiCad netlists (`kicad-cli sch export netlist`) at `0b59ece6` and at HEAD and compared them (`nl.py`).

**Netlist diff `0b59ece6` -> HEAD** (455 -> 444 components):
- Value changes: **none** among the 443 common references. MPN, LCSC and maker fields: none changed.
- Removed: 12 test points TP203, TP520, TP521, TP524, TP601, TP672, TP701, TP711, TP822, TP823, TP862, TP863 (probe pads only; `sim_exclude`, no conditioning effect; their nets are now probed at part pads, `bring-up-provision.md`). Added: NT601 (net tie).
- Net changes: R604.2, R606.2, U601.5, F602.2, C605.2, C606.2, C607.2, C608.2 moved from NEUTRAL to the new net AMC_HGND, joined to NEUTRAL at NT601 (commit `9ba2f45e`). The E decks already model that node as NEUTRAL, one potential (`circuit-proof.md` line 336). **Proof still holds**: only a net-tie copper resistance is added, far under the 750 ohm and 49.9 ohm legs.
- Footprint changes: T701, T711 `TO-252-3_TabPin2` -> `main-board:TO-252-2_TabPin2_Triac` (two-lead land, paste windows 63 % of the tab). The thermal row (CP 307, Tj 122.8 degC at typical Rth 75 K/W) was already **no** and is not re-derived for the new copper.

The pre-order check lists no unconnected-item or shorting rule among its violation counts.
