# Owner sign-off list: Sunbeam EM7000 main controller board, version 2

This is the design owner's record of every decision made on the project owner's behalf while the board was designed, laid out and checked, handed over in one piece. Every entry was decided by the design owner and signed on the project owner's behalf under his standing instruction of 2026-11-07, unless it is marked "the owner's own decision". On 2026-11-14 the project owner also asked not to be asked design, layer-stack or best-practice questions; the design owner ranked safety first, then neatness and workmanship, then the performance rules that matter on this board, then cost, and stopped only for a figure it could not meet. None of these entries relaxes a mains separation figure of the signed separation basis record (`hardware/sunbeam-em7000/separation-basis.yaml`): the 3.0 mm clearance and the 10.0 mm creepage were held throughout. The 6 kA fuse breaking capacity was not held by every fuse: the entry fuse F101 is short of it on the CQC and TUV figure (S-9). The risk acceptances of earlier rounds stand where they are recorded, in `open-items.md` and `DEC-MAIN-BOARD-RESIDUAL-RISKS-ACCEPTED-BY-OWNER`; this list adds two, both recorded in that decision on 2026-11-15: the hot relay pick-up (S-67, clause (z17)) and the branch fuses' breaking capacity on the CQC and TUV figure (S-9, clause (m)).

The board state described is the finished board of 2026-11-15, after the mains side was re-laid on 2026-11-14 and the optocoupler footprints were corrected on 2026-11-15. The signed acceptance lines themselves are in `workmanship-acceptance.yaml` and `severity-policy.yaml`; this list summarises them by class. Where a figure in an earlier working draft differed from the records, the records are followed.

## Look at these first

- S-1, S-2: the three optocouplers (U602, U701, U711) had footprints numbered as the mirror image of the parts. They were renumbered to the makers' numbers and two independent readings against the makers' sheets found the result right. It touches the mains side and a safety part.
- S-56: the assembler's placement rotation for those three parts must be read again when the order files are made, because pin 1 moved to the opposite corner of each footprint.
- S-9: the entry fuse F101 does not meet the 6 kA breaking capacity the records assume. The maker's sheet gives 10 kA at 250 V AC on the UL 248-14 figure (covers it) and 2 kA on the CQC and TUV figure, 3.0 times short; which body governs is not declared. The owner accepted this as a residual risk on 2026-11-05 (clause (m) of `DEC-MAIN-BOARD-RESIDUAL-RISKS-ACCEPTED-BY-OWNER`).
- S-67: K201 and the valve relays K731 and K741 may not pull in when hot, and no part closes it. Both parts stay under an accepted functional residual risk (the safe state is open), on two firmware rules and one bring-up bench test.
- S-8: the entry fuse F101 at 10 A against 9.2 A derated at 70 C air is accepted (clause (x) of the residual-risks decision); 10.96 A is the bound of a firmware failure, not a duty.
- S-3: the pump triacs T701 and T711 were read right at about 90 percent, not higher, because their sheet shows the lead order only in a picture.
- S-6, S-40: the pump snubbers were replaced by TVS clamps D701 and D711, with the copper from each clamp to its triac tab longer than the aim (27.5 mm and 18.6 mm against 15 mm). The coffee pump triac's worst-case junction reading is 110.1 C against a 110 C limit.
- S-52: signing the 64 test points nearer than 1 mm to a courtyard reverses the written position of the records, that every test point stays reachable. It is signed as met in substance for a bench bring-up of a one-off board.
- S-50, S-48: U401's capacitor C402 reaches its pin through a via, about 2 nH in the loop; three crystal-net pad-corner lines are signed where the records had kept the strict form; three U601 decoupling vias stand on mains-referenced nets.
- S-46: silkscreen text down to 0.8 mm is the project owner's legibility decision, not the maker's figure; the maker's page still names 1.0 mm. The cost is a possibly faint designator.
- S-72: the labels LINE IN and NEUTRAL IN at the mains inlet were moved on the front silkscreen so that each stands beside its own tab, after a review found LINE IN readable as the neutral tab's label. Check at bring-up that the line tab J102 and the neutral tab J103 carry the leads their labels name.
- S-62: thin stock and an unconfirmed assembler property. U401 stock fell from 7584 to 36, K201 has 20, T701 and T711 68, R105's price rose from 0.58 to 1.31 USD, and the maker's public record does not say whether the TVS clamp part is machine placed. The catalogue read expires on 2026-11-28.
- S-49: the coffee pump LED anode net is 1.65 times its shortest tree against a limit of 1.5, because its pad now sits behind its neighbours.
- S-5, S-59: U520's replacement part has a pin table that disagrees with its own drawing; the drawing matches the board. Meter-check pin 1 and pin 4 on the first board.
- S-42: the south edge is straight at 131.0 x 125.6 mm; the owner's own decision, with the south wall 0.9 mm away at worst tolerance.
- S-63 to S-66 and S-68: earlier entries that could not be shown to still hold are listed at the end. The board settled the capacitor distances (S-63, none moved; signed on behalf 2026-11-17, S-78) and the reed box (S-65); the rest wait for the owner.

## Part moves and part changes

- S-1. The footprints of U602 (LTV-814S, four pins) and U701 and U711 (MOC3052SVM, six pins) were renumbered to the makers' numbers, because the old numbering was the mirror image of the parts. The interchangeable pins were swapped in the symbols so that no mains copper moved (all 266 mains items identical, no new low-voltage copper within 3 mm of mains); low-voltage copper was re-laid at the three parts. Reason: a footprint numbered the wrong way round would put the optocoupler's LED, output and unused pins on the wrong pads.
- S-2. Two independent readings against the makers' sheets (Lite-On LTV-814 sheet, onsemi MOC3051M/52M/53M sheet) found U602, U701 and U711 right, with about 92 percent confidence. What limits it: the tape-and-reel pocket orientation, the 3D model and the assembler's own footprint for the MOC part were not checked. The MOC pin names in the symbol are blank for pins 1, 2, 4 and 6, which is cosmetic. The pre-order check now fails a footprint numbered clockwise.
- S-3. The four groups of three-terminal parts that check cannot judge were read against the makers' sheets and found right: BAT54SW (seven diodes, 98 percent), the SOT-223 regulator U302 (98 percent), BAS116 (four diodes, 97 percent) and the DPAK triacs T701 and T711 (90 percent). The symbols for the BAS116 and the triacs carry leftover names of other parts (BAS16W, BT136-600); pin numbers and functions are right.
- S-4. R522, R572 and R576 carry do-not-populate again, and so do R643 and R648, the temperature converters' unused force links (fitted, with R642 and R647 fitted, they would tie the force line to ground). Each is marked in three places that agree: the board footprint's do-not-populate attribute, the schematic symbol's do-not-populate attribute (which the ordered parts list reads; until 2026-11-15 the five symbols carried only a text field named dnp, which the parts list does not read) and `fitted: "no"` in `parts-specification.yaml`, with RT301, the inrush thermistor left unfitted behind the link JP303. The pre-order check now fails any part the three do not mark alike (`dnp_agreement`), and no acceptance waives it. Reason: the flow input is carried by the buffer U520 and R545, and R572 and R576 are burden positions that are not used.
- S-5. U520 is the UMW 74LVC1G17 (LCSC C7394034, stock 3125) instead of the Texas Instruments part (stock 11): same land and pinout, same thresholds. The UMW sheet's pin table swaps pins 4 and 5 against its own drawing; the drawing matches the Texas Instruments part and the board.
- S-6. The pump snubbers were replaced by one bidirectional TVS clamp across each pump triac, from its load terminal to neutral: D701 on the coffee pump at (25.40, 12.07) and D711 on the steam pump at (29.60, 5.36), Littelfuse SMCJ400CA (LCSC C151285), pad gap 4.30 mm. This was the owner's own decision of 2026-11-14. The proof distinguishes two clamp levels, both under the optocoupler triac's 600 V: at turn-off the clamp holds the pump parts to at most 527 V (1.14 under 600 V), and a mains surge reaching them through the pump coil is held to about 556 V, interpolated (1.08 under 600 V). The governing figure is the larger, 556 V, which is interpolated and not a maker figure, so the finding stays open in the proof. The clamp's figures come from the maker's listing and the pump coil's inductance is not measured.
- S-7. The pump triacs are the ST T835-800B, 800 V, 8 A (LCSC C2971290) in the same DPAK footprint as the 600 V part they replaced; no layout change. Reason: more voltage margin for the same board area.
- S-8. The entry fuse F101 is the soldered, tailed SETfuse SCT520PT10A250V, 10 A to match the 10 A plug, instead of fuse clips rated 10 A, which the 10.96 A of every load on in 70 C air would exceed. That 10.96 A is the bound of a firmware failure and not a permitted steady state: `REQ-POWER-BUDGET-001` caps the commanded draw at the outlet's 10 A at the worst of the supply span. The fuse has the same shortfall the clips had against the 9.2 A its derating gives at 70 C air (10 A x 0.92): clause (x) of `DEC-MAIN-BOARD-RESIDUAL-RISKS-ACCEPTED-BY-OWNER` (2026-11-06) accepts the 10 A fuse against 9.2 A derated, opening early being the safe direction, and everything behind it is rated for the 15 A band, so a firmware failure ends in the fuse opening or ageing. The circuit proof's F101 duty row gives the figures; the bring-up test of both elements fully on for 30 minutes in the closed enclosure (S-57) measures it. The owner preferred a replaceable fuse but accepted soldered, so that a one-shot build does not depend on a bring-up heat test. Pad pitch 28.0 mm (the maker's bend table is ambiguous and a real bend radius gives 28.0, at 1.5 mm of length), pads 3.0 mm round with a 1.1 mm hole and a 0.95 mm ring, copper at pad 2 of 4.38 mm against the rule figure of 4.2.
- S-9. The branch fuse holders F701, F711 and F731 get plated slots of 1.1 x 2.2 mm in 1.6 x 3.2 mm pads, which the maker makes without a special quote; a round 2.1 mm hole would leave the 0.35 mm leg loose. F731 stays the 5 x 20 holder and sits on the front as the third holder of the column, because the records ask 6 kA of every fuse and smaller stocked fuses give 35 to 150 A. Against that figure the branch fuses F701, F711 and F731 (SCT520, 0.4 to 10 A) meet it on the UL figure (10 kA at 250 V AC) and are 1.2 times short on the CQC and TUV figure (5 kA); the entry fuse F101 meets it on the UL figure (10 kA) and is 3.0 times short on the CQC and TUV figure (2 kA at 250 V AC). Both shortfalls are carried in the circuit proof as reported, not relaxed; the owner accepted the F101 shortfall as a residual risk on 2026-11-05 (clause (m) of `DEC-MAIN-BOARD-RESIDUAL-RISKS-ACCEPTED-BY-OWNER`), on the reasoning that the outlet's fault current is usually far below 6 kA. Clause (m) of that decision now carries the branch fuses' 1.2 times shortfall too, accepted on the same ground on 2026-11-15 by the design owner on the project owner's behalf.
- S-10. Supply-column moves so that the inner neutral pour is one piece: JP303 pad 1 (17.25, 50.0), RT301 pad 1 (26.55, 61.63) turned, R631 (19.338, 56.18), R630 (25.27, 65.70), R601 (25.27, 71.66), R602 (25.27, 78.20), TP606 re-sited, the free text LINK_MOD moved. Reason: one-piece neutral copper and a legal lane for the valve net.
- S-11. Measure island: TP604 to (46.0, 92.9) on the back, TP603 at (38.7, 80.0), C602 0.3 mm south, F602 stays on the front at (52.12, 79.39) because a back-side F602 would have been the only fitted back-side part. C606 changes from 0603 to 0402 (1 nF 50 V X7R, LCSC C1523) and moves to (50.295, 80.33) to make the room. HLDO_IN takes three vias and a short back lane. F602's designator stands inside the U301 courtyard.
- S-12. J622, J623, J627 and J628 carry the 1 x 2 header number C492401; they had carried a 40-pin strip number.
- S-13. The reed J862 is left out of the placement file and the parts list. The body sits on the back with the leads through to the front, soldered on the front. A fitting note says to measure the lead and spacing against the 1.0 mm holes at 20.0 mm before soldering.
- S-14. Low-voltage part moves of the layout rounds, all on the low-voltage side and none near mains copper, to shorten nets and keep lanes straight. The largest, checked against the board: D522 (I2C protection diode) moved about 30 mm to (110.4, 122.7) beside connector J521 where its line enters; C611 (the Y601 supply capacitor) about 56 mm to 3.3 mm from Y601 pin 4; C641 about 40 mm to 2.7 mm from U620 pin 20; C791 about 18 mm to 5.6 mm from U791 pin 20 (distances here are pad centre to pin centre on the final board); TP824 on the back at (116.05, 49.3). The rest are shifts of test points, pull-up and divider resistors and a few capacitors of a millimetre to a few millimetres onto their own lanes, in the converter, gauge and panel, controller-east, SWD and USB, ADC and heater-drive zones. The test points on the USB pair stand in line on the pair.
- S-15. R545 (220 ohm, LCSC C22962) was added in series between U520 pin 4 and the controller pin PB10, which closes a bootloader USART3_TX contention on the flow input.
- S-16. Seven controller pin swaps, which the project owner asked to be done and to review in the final pin map: WT1_DATA to pin 86, WT1_CLK to 87, WT2_DATA to 83, WT2_CLK to 84, BUZZER to pin 36 (PB2), AMC_CS to pin 77 (PA15, a debug pin at reset; its pull-up holds chip select high and the firmware must release it) and AMC_DRDY to pin 63 (PC6, interrupt line 6). Not swapped: ANODE_GATE (a timer channel), HEAT_S_DRV (noise beside current sensing) and GAUGE_STEP2.

## Relaxed rules

- S-17. Rule: slow lines stay on their own layer with few vias. Relaxed on the five key lines KEY1 to KEY5 (back layer under the controller's east pin column, three vias each on four of them), NTC_COF (one more via, replacing a 21 mm run on the wrong layer), AMC_RESET (one more via) and STM_SDA_X (two more vias, replacing a 12.75 mm wrong-layer run). This board does not need the rule there: these are switch levels, a slow temperature line, a static reset and slow I2C, and three ground planes lie between the back layer and the signal layers.
- S-18. Rule: matched length of a USB pair. Relaxed on the DM and DP pair to a difference of 1.4 mm, about 9 ps. This board does not need it: the port is full speed (12 Mbit/s), and the pair is still routed tight and parallel with its ESD part at the connector.
- S-19. Rule: via and stub limits on SWDIO, SWCLK and SWO, and up to three vias per gauge channel. This board does not need them: the debug lines are used on the bench only and the gauge signals are slow.
- S-20. Rule: no three-way junctions. Relaxed for plain square T branches on supply, ground and slow nets, with a waiver in `workmanship-acceptance.yaml`. Fast, sense, crystal and USB nets keep the strict form. A T on a supply rail has no signal-integrity effect.
- S-21. Rule: segment count and copper-to-length ratio. Exceptions for the supply mesh nets +3V3, +5V, GND, +3V3A and +5V_MOD, where many pads and 0.2 mm branches force the copper. A power mesh is judged by tidiness, not by segment count.
- S-22. Rule: crystal pad corners kept strict. Signed on LSE_IN, LSE_OUT and HSE_OUT (one line each), because the geometry adds well under 1 pF to a node that the load capacitors already set at several picofarads.
- S-23. The original bounds of at most 380 vias and 5250 mm of copper were abandoned, because closing the open joins needed more copper than assumed. The nets are judged by the per-net checks and signed entries below.
- S-24. Minimum track run 0.5 mm (it was 1 mm), and 0.2 mm tracks allowed within 2 mm of fine-pitch pads and at pad entries. The 135 degree corner rule is kept. Via annular ring 0.15 mm, the maker's minimum with no slack: it is the ring of the 466 ordinary vias of 0.6 mm pad over a 0.3 mm drill (the board's other vias, 6 of 0.7 mm and 69 of 0.75 mm, have 0.20 and 0.225 mm). Every via on the board has a 0.3 mm drill; no 0.2 mm drill is placed, which would cost about 50 USD and force the maker's four-wire test, and `fabrication-notes.md` and `design-rules.md` state the same. U401 ground pins 10, 19, 26, 49, 74 and 99 may have 0.2 mm stubs inside the courtyard.
- S-25. Kept strictly, not relaxed: mains clearance and creepage; crystal path limits (S-27); sense and reference integrity (no hooks or loops on sense nets); USB pair basics and ESD; digital decoupling closeness except as listed in S-50 and S-63; SPI3 as written; connector pin order on J791 and J821 and the reed J862; every low-voltage test point reachable except as S-52 states.
