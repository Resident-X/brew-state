# Design rules of the Sunbeam EM7000 main controller board

Every design rule and net class the main board uses, with its figure, where it is set in KiCad, where the figure comes from, and what stands behind it. The board is four layers at 1 oz finished outer copper, made by JLCPCB, as `manufacturer-limits.yaml` states. The rows below are the two board-wide minimums set before placement, because the placement view takes its resolution from them; the other rows are written at the rules step, before any track is routed (commit `5894bbcd` and the commits after it on this branch).

The board carries mains: its project text variable `MAINS` is `present`, the 40 nets of `layout-plan.md` MAINS-1 and the three no-connect pin nets of U601, U701 and U711 (row `No-connect pins in Mains` below) are in the `Mains` net class (and, for the 13 that carry a load's current, in `MainsTrunk` or `MainsBranch` as well), and the one `Mains separation` rule in `main-board.kicad_dru` is in the form the method asks. KiCad's read of the rules file and of each class's nets is quoted below from `task hw:rules-confirm`, run with `--class Default --class Mains --class Mains_Entry --class MainsTrunk --class MainsBranch --class Supply5V --class Supply3V3 --class Ground --class Sense --class Noisy --class Fast`: `RESULT | CONFIRMED | KiCad loads main-board.kicad_dru and places nets in each of Default, Mains, Mains_Entry, MainsTrunk, MainsBranch, Supply5V, Supply3V3, Ground, Sense, Noisy, Fast`. Every net is in one of `Supply5V`, `Supply3V3`, `Ground`, `Sense`, `Noisy` and `Fast` or none of them, never in two; the `Mains` nets alone are in more than one class, `MainsTrunk`, `MainsBranch` and `Mains_Entry` each being a narrower class of `Mains` nets as the rows below state.
