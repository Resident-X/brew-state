# Open items: Sunbeam EM7000 main controller board, schematic specification and proofs

Only what the graph, the records and the datasheets could not settle: an owner-held fact, a source that has to be amended, or a choice between workable options. Each item gives the question, the evidence and a recommendation. Everything else is settled in `schematic-spec.yaml`, `circuit-proof.md`, `circuit-verdicts.md` and `bring-up-provision.md`.

## Owner risk acceptances (2026-10-29)

Accepted by the owner in session on 2026-10-29 ("agree, proceed. I am signing that off, sign on my behalf"). The separation basis (`hardware/sunbeam-em7000/separation-basis.yaml`) was re-signed on the same instruction with the interlock-relay and isolated-measurement barriers added. Each acceptance is the owner's, with the owner's reason.

- **(a) The safety relay K201 welding shut.** Accepted as bounded by an independent element: the series overheat thermostats now, and the one-shot non-restoring thermal cutoff, owed and to be fitted before mains is first connected (DEL-BENCH-SAFETY-CHAIN-INSPECTION C7 and DEL-HW-BRING-UP C4). The firmware startup confirmation (REQ-ACTUATION-CONFIRMATION-001.C6) is a best-effort layer and does not count as detection. Reason: a welded contact is bounded by the independent element, so no second relay and no hardware detector are to be added.

- **(e) Bought-part creepage below the 10.0 mm board figure,** accepted at the maker's figure: U601 AMC131M02 8.0 mm (SBASA56, September 2023); K201 HF115F-Q 'over 8 mm' and K731/K741 HF32FA-G 'over 8 mm' (Hongfa); U701/U711 MOC3052SVM (the MOC3052M family's surface-mount lead-bend part, LCSC C899826, replacing C8965 on 2026-10-29) at least 7 mm (onsemi MOC3052M/D Rev 4; the figure is the family's and the same in Rev 3 of May 2019, the sheet LCSC lists for the SVM; the surface-mount part stays: the owner decided on 2026-10-29 against the through-hole 0.4 inch lead-spacing option, which adds assembly cost and hand soldering, accepting the 7 mm because the box is mostly closed and a coat is planned after test); U602 LTV-814S and the K201 clearance unknown until read from the makers. ## USB programming port (owner decision of 2026-10-29, graph criterion C15)

The owner decided to add a protected USB programming port, because the board will sit behind panels and its firmware must be reloadable without a probe or opening the machine: a five-way panel-cable header J402 (D+, D-, GND, shield, VBUS) and the ESD clamp D401, USBLC6-2SC6 (LCSC C7519, extended). The serial-wire header J401 is unchanged. A second owner decision of 2026-10-29 ("I want mains off, just board on a bench to load and debug firmware" and "safe if mains was added, so that my computer can't be damaged") has VBUS powering the board for bench work: VBUS -> D402 (SMF6.0A, C207260) -> F401 (MF-MSMF050-2, C17313) -> USB_VBUS_F (TP413) -> pin 3 of the 5 V source selector JP301 (XFCN PZ254V-11-03P, C2937625, one shorting jumper: pins 1-2 the module as drawn, pins 2-3 the cable, never both; the selector alone stops the cable and the module feeding each other and the board never sources current into the cable, as the module side is open in the cable position and the cable side is open in the module position). The bench state is the owner's: only the controller, its regulators, the indicators it carries and the debug and USB parts powered, with no sensor head, gauge, load or mains. The 3.3 V regulator U302 is the LT1963AEST-3.3#TRPBF (Analog Devices, C459918, extended). It replaced the AP7361C-33ER-13 on 2026-10-29 (round-1 finding 1.1): that part's 6.5 V absolute maximum input is under the logic module's 6.75 V over-voltage trip, and the AMS1117 before it could not regulate on the cable's 4.75 V minimum minus the fuse's drop (the LT1963A's dropout is 0.35 V at 500 mA). The rows are `USB power` in `circuit-proof.md`.

**Settled (PA9 and VBUS).** Device mode works without PA9. RM0433 Rev 8 57.14.1 gives OTG_GOTGCTL BVALOEN and BVALOVAL, which override the B-session-valid signal, and OTG_GCCFG bit 21 VBDEN, where 0 is "VBUS detection disabled"; RCC USBSEL = 11 selects hsi48_ck as the USB kernel clock. The ST bootloader's DFU uses PA11 and PA12 only and clocks USB from HSI48 with CRS (AN2606 Rev 70, 60.1 Table 135); it drives PA9 as USART1 TX (alternate push-pull, pull-up; an input until USART1 is detected, only in bootloader 0x91) and PA8 as I2C3 SCL (open-drain, no pull). PA9 stays the coffee pump gate. With BOOT0 high no proof-of-life reaches the interlock, so no pump has a live feed whatever PA9 does.

## Bring-up measurements carried (DEC-UNPUBLISHED-MAKER-FIGURES-CARRIED-TO-BRING-UP)

Each figure below is one a part's maker does not publish and a record of this board relies on. It is carried to bring-up as a measurement on the built board against a limit stated here, before the boards are ordered; a reading outside the limit fails bring-up and the part or the limit is revisited with the owner (`DEL-HW-BRING-UP.C15`). The part is graded on the range its maker does publish: a row never stretches a rating, never concerns a safety barrier's rating, and never closes an absolute maximum the part's operation needs (row M1 says where that applies). Each reading, the limit and the result are recorded against the finding in the last column. The bring-up steps are the rows of `bring-up/*.yaml` that name the row number, rendered in `bring-up-provision.md`.

Instruments are those of a home bench: multimeter, bench supply with current limit, oscilloscope (earth-referenced; a mains-referenced node is read only with an isolated or differential probe, never an earthed lead), LCR meter, thermocouple, and the board's own measurement channels. Two facts bound them. A bench LCR meter applies no DC bias, so its reading is the unbiased value. No surge generator is held, so a transient is not generated; where the transient is what the figure is about, the cell says **worst case assumed** with the value and its source. The **charge-time method** reads the capacitance of a part at its working voltage: the bench supply set to the working voltage charges the part through a resistor R (1 kohm for 100 nF and 1 uF parts, 100 ohm for 10 uF; R read with the multimeter), the scope reads the time t to 63.2 % of the supply, and the capacitance is t / R (a secant value, at or above the differential value at that bias, so a limit compared with it carries a stated margin where it is not an exact floor).

"Before mains" means the board unpowered or on the bench supply with mains disconnected at the plug; a row marked so is done before mains is first connected, as `DEL-HW-BRING-UP.C15` requires for a mains-connected circuit. "First current-limited connection" means the first connection of mains through the lamp at JP101, for a figure that can only be read with mains present.
