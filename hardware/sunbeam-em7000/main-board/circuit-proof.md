# Circuit proof: Sunbeam EM7000 main controller board

The record `docs/board-design-method.md` (section "Proving value-critical circuits before layout") requires before any footprint is placed: one row for every value-critical circuit, with its figure, where the figure comes from, how the circuit is shown to meet it under the worst conditions it will see, and whether it does. This is that record for the main board, for the parts and values of `schematic-spec.yaml`.

## How to read it

- **Met** is `yes` only when the result meets the figure under every condition the row lists. A row is **NO** when its source does not bound a figure the row needs, or when the circuit does not meet the figure; the amendment that closes it is written in the row's Met cell. No figure here was filled in where its source is silent, and no distance or rating was relaxed.
- Simulations are ngspice 47 decks in `circuit-proof/` (`*.cir`, with the run's `*.log` beside it: each ran with exit status 0 and printed its `.meas` line; a log with a line beginning `Error` holds no result). Scripts (`*.py`) print their arithmetic into the matching `*.log`/`*.out`. There is no KiCad schematic yet, so every deck is a standalone netlist whose reference designators and values are those of `schematic-spec.yaml`; the schematic, once drawn, is exported as a SPICE netlist into `circuit-proof/` and each deck is re-run against it and compared with the committed netlist before placement. In SPICE and KiCad's simulator `M` means milli: values of a megohm are written `Meg`.
- Datasheets and manufacturer pages were read on 2026-10-29 (the dates in a row are the dates of the document read, its revision, and its table or graph); they are cited by maker, document number, revision and table, and by address. Downloaded documents are not committed. The enclosure air is the constraints record's assumed 70 degC (`docs/sunbeam-em7000/controller-board-design-constraints.md`, Enclosure air temperature), an assumption that bring-up measures.
- Copper is 1 oz, the manufacturer's intro-price default. A conductor row that needs the fuse's let-through and the laminate's temperatures stays NO until both are sourced; the arithmetic is shown for the day they are.
- The sections B-pin-table (`controller-pin-table.md`), the hold-off facts and the per-load hold-off tables below are the inputs and the proof of the hold-off for every load the board switches.
- The five parts below were prepared per group of sheets (A mains entry and interlock, B controller and logic supply, C sensing front ends, D drives and low-voltage inputs/outputs, E measurement and converters) and are kept whole; references like "owner A" in them mean the part that owns that sheet group.

## A. Mains entry and interlock

Decks, scripts and logs are in `circuit-proof/` (prefix `A_`). Re-run: `circuit-proof/A_run_all.sh` (regenerates `A_*.cir` with `A_gen_decks.py`, runs ngspice on each, every run exits 0 and prints its `.meas` lines), then `python3 -I A_results.py`, `python3 -I A_hold_limit.py`, `python3 -I A_calcs.py`. Every deck's parts, nets and values are those of `sheets/102-interlock.yaml` (`A_interlock.cir`); SPICE line names: `XU201`=U201, `MQ201`=Q201, `DZ201`=Z201; K201, test points and tabs are excluded from simulation and the relay coil is added in each deck as a load. In SPICE and KiCad's simulator `5.6M` is milli: the sheets write `5.6Meg` and `1Meg`.

Result in one line: the interlock's rest-state removal, the coil clamp and the pick-up are shown; **the record's 19-part drive does NOT remove in the held-high rest state (gate decay 1244 ms, not under 70 ms): the sheet adds R205 and R206 (100 kohm, 21 parts) and with them the state removes in 56.5 ms.** The timeout-against-refresh row FAILS on the datasheet limits of gate and diode leakage; the coil inductance is open; no conductor row is met because the fuse sheet gives no let-through and no source gives the copper thickness, theta_i or theta_f.

The STM32 corners (240 Hz and 344 Hz) are above the Nyquist frequency of the 100 Hz sample rate (50 Hz): RCFL-1 asks the corner to pass the band, not to prevent aliasing, and these filters do not prevent it; mains pickup between 50 Hz and the corner folds into the band, and what removes it is the firmware's low-pass, which no record fixes. The MAX31865 rejects 50 or 60 Hz in its own converter (82 dB, Electrical Characteristics, 50/60 Hz Noise Rejection). The HX711's sampling capacitor and source resistance are not stated by the maker (Avia V2.0), so for WT1_INA_N, WT1_INA_P, WT2_INA_N and WT2_INA_P the item's clause on the sampling capacitor cannot be shown and those four stay failing.

### F3. Inlet filter C101: emission target and attenuation (finding 4.20, EMSRC-1)

C101 is the KNSCHA MPX104K31C3KN15600 X2 capacitor, 0.1 uF +-10 %, 310 VAC (specification for approval 2026.3.25; section A): line to neutral only, no line-to-earth capacitor, no choke. The sheet gives capacitance and approvals, no attenuation or impedance curve.

- **Attenuation, estimated.** An ideal capacitor across the line, in a 50 ohm and 50 ohm measuring network (100 ohm differential), has reactance 10.6 ohm at 150 kHz, 3.2 ohm at 500 kHz, 0.32 ohm at 5 MHz. The differential-mode attenuation is X / sqrt(100^2 + X^2): 19.5 dB at 150 kHz, 29.9 dB at 500 kHz, 49.9 dB at 5 MHz. The capacitor's series inductance is not in the sheet, so the figure is an upper estimate of the attenuation at the high end and is not a maker's figure. Common-mode attenuation is nil: there is no earth capacitor, by decision (the constraints record § Mains entry; `TOUCH-1` in F5 keeps the leakage to earth at nothing from this board).
- **Emission limit.** The standard is named, AS/NZS CISPR 14.1 (constraints record); the limit rows are not held and no free source with them was found on 2026-10-29 (F2), so no limit value is written here.
- **Target (the owner's, declared):** best effort. No emissions test is planned for this machine; the entry carries an X2 filter, the module carries its own Class B filter (F2), and the board's own dominant source, the pump triacs' phase-angle edge, is attenuated line to line by C101 as estimated above. Conformance to CISPR 14.1 is not claimed, and is the thing an adopter who must sell the machine tests. This is a recorded reason, not a limit met: the item's clause "a record naming no limit fails" is not satisfied by it.

### F5. Touch current of the logic supply module to the touchable secondary (finding 4.19, TOUCH-1)

- **Standard and class.** The machine's metal parts are bonded to protective earth (`separation-basis.yaml`, pressure-sensor barrier; `reference-machine.md`), so it is a Class I appliance; it is a benchtop appliance a person moves, taken as **portable**. The limit is that of IEC 60335-1:2010, Class I portable appliances, 0.75 mA (Cosel, *Leakage current*, https://www.cosel.com/en/coselplus/technical/109, the table of limits by appliance type, read 2026-10-29; the page is a secondary source quoting the standard, gives no measurement conditions and does not show the Class II rows; the standard's text is not held). Stationary Class I motor-operated appliances are 3.5 mA on the same page; the lower figure is taken. **Assumed:** Class I portable; the owner confirms the class.
- **Which current.** The touch current is the current through a person touching the USB connector or the other touchable low-voltage parts (`separation-basis.yaml`, touchable: USB connector, operator controls, enclosure). U301's secondary is not earthed (Class II module, output not referenced to earth), so U301's leakage reaches a person there. The protective-conductor current is a separate quantity: this board connects nothing from a mains conductor to protective earth (C101 is line to neutral, no Y capacitor), so it adds none; the earlier board's 373 uA filter leakage does not arise (constraints record, § Mains entry protection).
- **Estimate.** The maker states leakage under 0.25 mA at 277 Vac (IRM-20-SPEC 2025-11-21, Input), frequency and conditions not stated. Leakage through the module's capacitance scales with voltage and frequency: at 253 V, 0.25 x 253 / 277 = 0.228 mA at the sheet's frequency; if the sheet's figure is at 50 Hz and the supply is 60 Hz (the forms record's alternate), x 1.2 = 0.274 mA; with a further **assumed** 20 % for the ceramic capacitors' rise with voltage and frequency (Schaffner, *Leakage Currents in Power Line Filters*, July 2008, section 3, as the method cites) = 0.329 mA. Other parts: the barrier parts (optocouplers and relays) couple through pF-scale capacitance, taken as **assumed** 10 pF each: 2 pi x 60 Hz x 10 pF x 253 V = 0.95 uA each, under 10 uA for the ten of them. The total, 0.34 mA, against 0.75 mA, is 2.2 times under the limit.
- **What the figure rests on.** The module's own sheet figure is the maker's, "under" and not a measurement; the limit is quoted from a secondary source; the measuring network of the standard (weighted) is not applied, and the unweighted figure is an upper estimate at mains frequency. The bring-up measurement of the leakage of the finished machine is the check on all of these.
