# Layout plan of the Sunbeam EM7000 main controller board

**Naming note (2026-11-17).** This plan was written while the board was called v2 and its folder was `main-board-v2/`. The board's earlier design (called v1 then, "the first design" below) was frozen and never reached `main`, so the board is now called v1 and its folder is `main-board/`; `DEC-BOARD-VERSION-NAMING-V2-WORKING-NAME` records why. "The second design" and "the first design" below keep their historical meaning (this board, and the earlier frozen design it replaced); "v2" in a render folder name or a dated record means this board.

This is the floorplan and the plan for the main controller board of the Sunbeam EM7000, written before any footprint is placed, as `docs/board-design-method.md` § Planning the layout asks. It is the plan of the board's second design, the one in this folder, `main-board/` (called `main-board-v2/` while the working name v2 stood): the low-voltage half is re-placed to candidate A, the placement the Phase 1b and Phase 1c scratch trials settled (`hardware/sunbeam-em7000/main-board/PLAN.md`, Phase 1b outcome and Phase 1c outcome), and the mains half is carried unchanged from the first design (commit e8615c82, marked by the local, unpublished git tag `main-board-v1-final`, whose layout plan lived at `hardware/sunbeam-em7000/main-board/layout-plan.md`). The board is six layers (F.Cu, In1.Cu to In4.Cu, B.Cu), 131.0 mm by 125.6 mm, outline from (1, 2) to (132, 127.6) in board coordinates, 1.6 mm thick, 1 oz outer copper, ENIG (JLCPCB offers HASL only below six layers), fixed at the machine's four mounting points (`docs/sunbeam-em7000/controller-board-division.md`, § The main board's floorplan).

**Coordinates.** Every coordinate here is the board editor's: x to the right, y down. The division record's datum has Y up from the cavity's lower left, so a position taken from it keeps its x and takes y = 129 − Y (its Y 2.0 to 127.0 becomes y 127 to 2, its bottom edge the board's bottom edge). The left edge is the edge the mains harness enters, the right edge the one the low-voltage harness enters.

**Stack.** Six copper layers, 1.6 mm thick (`manufacturer-limits.yaml` states that JLCPCB offers 1.6 mm at six layers). F.Cu carries the components and signal tracks. In1.Cu is the solid `GND` plane under `low voltage`, `analog sensing` and `digital`, with nothing routed on it. In2.Cu is a `GND` fill stitched to In1.Cu that carries, outside `mains`, the signal tracks BOARD-18 permits and one supply pour, a `+3V3` pour (zone `3V3 pour In2`, net `+3V3`, outline (69, 17.5), (130.75, 17.5), (130.75, 44), (113, 44), (113, 92), (130.75, 92), (130.75, 126), (69, 126), notched at (69, 77), (77, 77), (77, 47.9), (69, 47.9), clearance 0.3 mm, minimum width 0.25 mm, priority above the `GND` fill, through-hole pads on thermal spokes, island removal on); the notch keeps the pour out of U301's barrier strip (MAINS-5) and the pour stands 10.30 mm or more from the nearest mains copper (BOARD-6) (`+5V` and `+3V3A` stay tracks on F.Cu and B.Cu; the `GND` fill stays where a via or through-hole pad ties it to ground) and, inside `mains` only, the routes of `Mains`-class nets that have no surface path (BOARD-5, BOARD-18). In3.Cu carries signal tracks outside `mains` and, inside `mains` only, two `Mains`-class runs that have no path on F.Cu, In2.Cu or B.Cu: `ZC_LED_A` (U602 pin 2 to R631), one run of about 56.5 mm at 0.25 mm with one via (0.6 mm, drill 0.3 mm) at each end, and `HOTVALVE` (under the `VALVE_COM` line on B.Cu), one run of 43.9 mm at 0.3 mm between its two through-hole pads with no via (BOARD-5). In3.Cu lies one prepreg ply under In2.Cu, so an In3.Cu mains run lies under In2.Cu copper only of its own net or of the neutral pour, the `HOTVALVE` overlap measured at 3.95 mm2 (at most 5 mm2) and the `ZC_LED_A` overlap at 13.1 mm2 (the same LED circuit, about 1.2 V across it), and crosses any other In2.Cu mains net only at right angles; this is the board's own rule, not a figure of the separation basis. In4.Cu is a `GND` fill stitched to In1.Cu that carries slow nets only, never a `Fast`, `Sense`, supply or `Mains` net. B.Cu carries tracks, a `GND` pour and all 84 test points (TEST-1, TEST-2), and no assembled part. **Ground neighbour, the board's own rule (a layout practice, not a mains figure or a figure of the separation basis):** every layer that carries signal tracks has a `GND` layer directly beside it on at least one side: F.Cu and In2.Cu beside In1.Cu, In3.Cu beside the In4.Cu `GND` fill and, inside the pour's outline on In2.Cu, beside the `+3V3` pour, B.Cu beside In4.Cu. That is why In4.Cu carries slow nets only: a `Fast` or `Sense` track there would leave In3.Cu and B.Cu with no ground beside them on that side and would sit over a fill cut by other tracks. **Mains separation, how the declared figures are met (BOARD-6; the owner accepted the basis on 2026-11-18):** every plane and pour on every layer stops from `Mains`-class copper by the two declared figures, 3.0 mm in a straight line in plan view and 10.0 mm along the shortest surface path that crosses no barrier slot, going round the slot ends (the six barrier slots are 1.2 mm wide, over the basis's 1.0 mm); copper may therefore stand nearer than 10.0 mm straight where a slot lengthens the path. This changes no declared figure. It replaces the single 10.0 mm straight-line pull-back, and the 10.6 mm straight-line fence the routing study used for new copper, that this paragraph carried before.

**Net names** are written without the sheet path KiCad's netlist prefixes to a label local to one sheet (`LINE_IN` for `/Mains entry and load feed/LINE_IN`). A net named in backquotes is one the netlist holds; a class named there (`Mains`, `MainsTrunk`, `MainsBranch`, `Supply5V`, `Supply3V3`, `Ground`, `Sense`, `Noisy`, `Fast`) is the board's own name, `Mains` being the only one the method fixes. `Mains` holds the 38 nets the schematic's specification lists; the other classes are the rules step's.

**Where this plan departs from the first design's floorplan.** The mains rectangles, and every part and test point they hold, are the first design's byte for byte, so the mains copper, the six barrier slots and the signed separation basis carry over unchanged. The low-voltage, analog sensing and digital rectangles are redrawn around candidate A, the placement the Phase 1c trial settled on 2026-11-10 (`hardware/sunbeam-em7000/main-board/PLAN.md`, Phase 1b outcome and Phase 1c outcome), so that every part of candidate A has its courtyard inside the regions that list it and no two regions overlap. In candidate A the controller U401 stands at (104, 79) turned 180 degrees, so its pins 1 to 25 face east (the crystals Y401 and Y402, the analogue supply filter and the east filter island), pins 26 to 50 face north (the ADC filter island), pins 51 to 75 face west (the drive stages between the controller and the barrier, the USB port J402 and the debug header J401) and pins 76 to 100 face south (the SPI3 bus, the converter selects and the load-cell clocks, toward the converter cell); the converter cell U620, U621, U630, U631 stands south of the controller; the analog headers and their filters stand north and north-east, in the band (80.2, 2)–(108, 48.4) that Phase 1b opened to `analog sensing`; the panel header J821 stands inside the board at (111.64, 31.04) with its drivers Q821, Q822 and R821 within 8 mm, the gauge header J791 and the wall-board header J761 by the right edge; the regulator U302 stands beside JP301 in its cell; and the interlock cell stands at the bottom, left of the converter cell. **Owner decisions of 2026-11-10 this floorplan records** (`PLAN.md`, Phase 1b and Phase 1c outcomes, and `BOARD-1`, `BOARD-2`, `BOARD-8`, `BOARD-11`, `BOARD-24`, `SENSE-5`, `CLOCK-4`, `FLOOR-3`, `FLOOR-5`): the sensing parts stand 17.5 mm or more from the bodies of the module U301 and the relay K201 (A8's 20 mm accepted down to the level the first design held); U401 stands at (104, 79) turned 180 degrees with its block about 13 mm from the module body (U401 itself 13.28 mm; the rest of its block 15.5 mm or more), not 15 mm; J791 and J821 may stand with pin 1 anywhere in the box x 104–129, y 2–34.5, pin 1 staying pin 1, the way order fixed and the rotation free; every other low-voltage connector is free, with no edge or cluster rule; the reed J862 stays at (122, 91.5) and the mains tab pads and the `mains` region stay where the first design has them; the crystals stand 10 mm or more from J761 and J862 (`CLOCK-4`); U302 stands beside JP301 in its cell; D401 and D402 stand beside J402; and Q821, Q822 and R821 stand within 8 mm of J821. The connector table gives candidate A's pin 1 positions. Because the placement interleaves the regions at the scale of one part, a part whose courtyard crosses a rectangle boundary, or stands within 0.05 mm of one, is listed in both regions it touches; the placement view reports a part outside the regions it is listed in, and a candidate placement leaves none.

**Placement guidance.** The Phase 4 candidate 1 (`PLAN.md`, Phase 4, step 2) is candidate A2, not candidate A. A2 is candidate A (`pl_a2s5`) with these parts moved and nothing else changed, found on 2026-11-10 after the confidence checks showed the HSE crystal nets too long in A: Y401 to (123.10, 77.90); C414 (HSE_IN load capacitor) to (117.21, 76.70); C415 (HSE_OUT) to (120.56, 81.20); C416 (LSE_IN) to (113.83, 86.67) turned 270 degrees; C417 (LSE_OUT) to (118.33, 83.67) turned 270 degrees; TP406 to (116.44, 76.30); the ADC filter capacitors C502 to (113.92, 76.25), C522 to (106.50, 68.33), C523 to (109.00, 69.08) and C524 to (117.42, 69.50); and the two parts those moves displaced, C411 to (113.95, 71.31) and R672 to (123.80, 83.06). Every connector, U401, U520, Y402, the reed box and every mains part stand where candidate A has them, so the connector table below is unchanged. Wherever a row below says "candidate A" about a figure A2 does not list as changed, the figure is A2's too.

**Rules this layout is held to** (they are the board's rules, written here so the placement is drawn to them rather than corrected to them; each has its row below or in `design-rules.md`): every corner is 135 degrees or wider and every run is 0.5 mm or longer, pad and via stubs included (`WORK-4`, `WORK-5`); the ground pin stubs of U401 (pads 10, 19, 26, 49, 74 and 99) may be 0.2 mm wide, not 0.45 mm, because a 0.45 mm stub cannot leave a 0.5 mm pitch pad at 0.2 mm clearance (`POWER-1`); the board's minimum track width and clearance are 0.09 mm (`MFG-1`); a 3.3 V track is 0.2 mm, not 0.3 mm, because a 0.3 mm track cannot leave a 0.5 mm pitch pad at 0.2 mm clearance; every ordinary via is 0.3 mm drill and 0.6 mm pad, and 0.2 mm drills are not chosen (the JLCPCB quote prices them as a capability, about 50 USD, and forces its four-wire test, and the Phase 1 escape study needed none); a via in an exposed thermal pad is capped with solder mask on the component side, as `MFG-3` allows, since JLCPCB fills only vias whose pad is 0.5 mm or less and a 0.6 mm pad is left open; no via stands in any other surface-mount pad (`BOARD-22`); every assembled part is on F.Cu and B.Cu carries test pads only (`BOARD-23`); the harness headers J791 and J821 keep their pin order, pin 1 being pin 1 and the way order fixed, and their sockets may stand at any angle (`BOARD-24`).

**Owner decisions this plan rests on or leaves open** are in `open-items.md` and the report that came with this file; they are not repeated as requirements here.
