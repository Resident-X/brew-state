# Layout plan of the Sunbeam EM7000 main controller board

This is the floorplan and the plan for the main controller board of the Sunbeam EM7000, written before any footprint is placed, as `docs/board-design-method.md` § Planning the layout asks. The board is four layers, 131.0 mm by 125.0 mm, outline from (1, 2) to (132, 127) in board coordinates, 1.6 mm thick, 1 oz outer copper, leaded HASL, fixed at the machine's four mounting points (`docs/sunbeam-em7000/controller-board-division.md`, § The main board's floorplan).

**Coordinates.** Every coordinate here is the board editor's: x to the right, y down. The division record's datum has Y up from the cavity's lower left, so a position taken from it keeps its x and takes y = 129 − Y (its Y 2.0 to 127.0 becomes y 127 to 2, its bottom edge the board's bottom edge). The left edge is the edge the mains harness enters, the right edge the one the low-voltage harness enters.

**Stack.** F.Cu carries the components and signal tracks; In1.Cu is the solid `GND` plane under `low voltage`, `analog sensing` and `digital`; In2.Cu is a `GND` fill stitched to In1.Cu that carries, outside `mains`, only the signal tracks BOARD-18 permits (`+5V` and `+3V3` are tracks on F.Cu and B.Cu, not pours) and, inside `mains` only, the mains routes that have no surface path (BOARD-5); B.Cu carries tracks, a `GND` pour and all 96 test points, and no assembled part. Every plane and pour stops 10.0 mm from mains copper on every layer (BOARD-6).

**Net names** are written without the sheet path KiCad's netlist prefixes to a label local to one sheet (`LINE_IN` for `/Mains entry and load feed/LINE_IN`). A net named in backquotes is one the netlist holds; a class named there (`Mains`, `MainsTrunk`, `MainsBranch`, `Supply5V`, `Supply3V3`, `Ground`, `Sense`, `Noisy`, `Fast`) is the board's own name, `Mains` being the only one the method fixes. `Mains` holds the 40 nets the schematic's specification lists; the other classes are the rules step's.

**Where this plan departs from the division record's block drawing** (the revision `open-items.md` owes it): the isolated converter U601 stands with its long axis across the slot between its pad rows below the module, mains row left, because the record's 5 mm strip cannot hold a 12.8 mm SOIC-20W; the digital headers J521 and J522 and the two I2C protection networks stand in a bottom-edge cell, not among the analog headers; the thermistor and sensor-input filters stand in an island against U401's converter pins; the USB port J402 and its clamps stand in a cluster at the top edge in `digital`, as the record's item 17 asks; and every test point is a pad on B.Cu. The four lid-screw holes H1 to H4, the three fiducials FID1 to FID3 and the second shunt test pads TP107 and TP108 are drawn in the schematic (456 parts in all, the net tie NT601 included) and are listed in the regions that hold them (BOARD-7, MFG-10, TEST-3); the two region rectangles (123, 2.3)–(131.7, 10) and (122.5, 116.5)–(131.7, 126.7) were added to `low voltage` and `digital` so that the holes at the right corners lie inside a region.

**Owner decisions this plan rests on or leaves open** are in `open-items.md` and the report that came with this file; they are not repeated as requirements here.
