# Fabrication notes: Sunbeam EM7000 heater wall board

It follows the main board's `fabrication-notes.md`, whose research it cites and does not repeat. ## Laminate and comparative tracking index

The requirement (C11): the notes prefer laminate families with a published comparative tracking index (CTI) of at least 175 (material group IIIa or better), as margin at pollution degree 2 and not as a precondition.

The separation basis credits pollution degree 2 on the material group III column, which covers groups IIIa and IIIb alike, so a laminate under 175 changes none of the basis's figures (`separation-basis.yaml`, condition 3). This board's laminate preference is therefore margin. It is the same preference as the main board's, and rests on the same research, which this file does not repeat: the main board's `fabrication-notes.md`, "Laminate and comparative tracking index" (read 2026-11-05) names the laminate makers' own datasheets and what the maker's pages say.

- **Preferred families, with a published CTI class and range of at least 175 (material group IIIa or better):** Shengyi S1000-2M / S1000-2MB (class PLC 3, specification "175~250", typical "200 V") and Shengyi S1000-2 / S1000-2B (PLC 3, "175 V to 249 V"), as the main board's notes record them from the laminate maker's sheets. A published figure of exactly 175 V sits on the group boundary with no margin; the notes say so.

  **Amended 2026-11-20 (the document and revision read, for each preferred family, as the main board's notes record them, read 2026-11-05 and not read again for this board):**
  - Shengyi S1000-2M / S1000-2MB: the laminate maker's "S1000-2M data sheet (20120321)" (file title), 3 pages, revision as printed 20120321 (the same text in a 2013-05 copy), General Properties table, page 1: CTI, IEC60112 method, V, specification "PLC3(175~250)", typical value "PLC3(200V)"; the sheet says its data are typical values and not specification values. Copy read: `https://a-contract.ru/fileadmin/datasheets/base-material/S1000-2M.pdf`, a distributor-hosted copy.
  - Shengyi S1000-2 / S1000-2B: the laminate maker's S1000-2 catalogue page "-31-", no revision printed, file created 2009, General Properties table, page 1: CTI, IEC60112 method, V, specification "PLC 3(175V--249V)", typical "PLC 3"; remark 3 says typical values are for reference. Copy read: `https://a-contract.ru/fileadmin/datasheets/base-material/S1000-2.pdf`, a distributor-hosted copy.
  - The copies are distributor-hosted; the laminate makers' own sites could not be reached for current revisions, so neither is shown to be the maker's current document (main board's notes, "Laminate makers' own datasheets (read 2026-11-05)", which also lists the families whose maker publishes no index, or only a typical value or no method, and that are not named as preferred: Shengyi S1141, Kingboard KB-6165F and KB-6165, Nan Ya NP-140TL).
- **The order note:** "laminate: Shengyi S1000-2M or S1000-2, comparative tracking index class PLC 3 (175 V or more) per IEC 60112; report any substitution before production". It is advice to the maker and not a requirement the maker is shown to honour: the maker's pages read on 2026-11-05 give a pool of laminate makers (Nan Ya, KB, Shengyi and others) and no way to fix one for an order.
- **The written request the owner makes of the manufacturer, stated as the main board's notes state it, for this order (**amended 2026-11-20**):** before ordering, the owner asks JLCPCB's customer support, in writing, for (1) the laminate maker and product that will be used for this two-layer order, with that maker's datasheet; (2) confirmation that it can be fixed to one of the two preferred families (S1000-2M or S1000-2) at order time, and the order-form field or note that does it (the order note above); (3) a material or conformance certificate for the shipped lot stating the CTI to IEC 60112, or the laminate maker's report. If the maker cannot fix the laminate or supply the certificate, the order is not held for it at pollution degree 2 (the main board's notes, "CTI research of 2026-11-05 and the order stance"). **No record in the repository shows this request has been sent, for either board: it is the owner's pre-order act and stays to be made.** Receiving the certificate is out of scope of criterion C11.
- **What remains unverified, and is a pre-order action for the owner:** the identity and CTI of the laminate the maker will ship for this two-layer order, until the maker answers the written request the main board's notes set out (the laminate maker and product with its datasheet; whether it can be fixed to one of the two families; a certificate of the shipped lot). A two-layer order may use a different laminate from the six-layer one; the request is made for this order as for that one.

## Conformal coat

The requirement (C10): the notes name the coat that supports the basis's pollution degree 2 credit (which rests on the enclosure), with its type and the maker's current datasheet, state that it is applied after bring-up once the machine is deemed functional from a hardware perspective, and say where it is applied.

The coat is a supporting measure and earns no part of the pollution degree 2 credit, which stands on the enclosure (`separation-basis.yaml`, conditions 1 and 2). No coating test is planned and none is claimed.

### Coat chosen

The same coat as the main board, so that one can serves both boards: a one-part acrylic conformal coating in an aerosol can, sprayed at home with no equipment beyond a ventilated space, cured at room temperature, removable by a common solvent or by soldering through it.

- **Product: MG Chemicals 419D Acrylic Conformal Coating, aerosol, part number 419D-340G** (410 mL, 340 g net). **Fallback, equivalent type: Techspray Turbo-Coat Acrylic Conformal Coating, aerosol, product 2108-12S.**
- **The maker's current datasheet:** "419D Aerosol, Acrylic Conformal Coating", technical data sheet, 3 pages, dated 31 July 2025, Ver. 4.0, `https://mgchemicals.com/downloads/tds/tds-419d-a.pdf`, as the main board's notes cite it (fetched 2026-11-17 there; not fetched again here). The figures relied on (dielectric strength, service temperature -65 to 125 degC against the 70 degC air assumed and the 45 degC working figure at the wall (**amended 2026-11-20**: that figure is superseded for the triacs' wall path by 25 degC nominal and 40 degC bound; 70 degC governs the board-side parts the coat covers), cure times, film thickness 25 to 75 um, rework by solder-through) are tabled there. The sheet states no creepage or clearance credit and no pollution degree, so none is taken; the cured film's chemical resistance is printed "Poor", so the enclosure and not the coat protects against solvent spills.
- **Flammability and supply:** the main board's notes carry the safety-data-sheet figures and the supply note; the owner reads the SDS packed with the can.

### Where and when it is applied

After bring-up, once the machine is deemed functional from a hardware perspective (owner, 2026-11-06). Until then the board is uncoated: the first connection of this board's mains side and the bring-up bench tests run on the uncoated board, in a clean room, and the coat is no part of the bound on any fault between mains pads until it is applied. The owner applies it at home.

It is applied to **both faces of the board**, covering the gaps between this board's mains pads: the pads of the nets `OUT_C`, `OUT_S`, `RET`, `HEAT_C_OPTO_MT`, `HEAT_S_OPTO_MT`, `HEAT_C_GATE_R`, `HEAT_S_GATE_R`, `HEAT_C_TRIAC_G` and `HEAT_S_TRIAC_G` (the Mains class) on the parts T911, T921, U911, U921, R911, R912, R921, R922, RV911, RV921, J911, J921 and J941, and in practice the whole board face except the keep-outs. The mains region is drawn (**amended 2026-11-19**, at layout): it is the whole board, outline (20.0, 21.0) to (65.0, 96.0) in board millimetres, except the two low-voltage rectangles of `layout-plan.md`, x 56.0 to 65.0 by y 50.19 to 66.21 round the header J931 and the low-voltage pins of U911 and U921, and x 55.0 to 65.0 by y 88.0 to 96.0 round the mounting hole H1. Both faces carry mains copper, so both are coated.

**Keep-outs for contacts, by reference designator** (**amended 2026-11-19**: confirmed against the drawn pads at layout and drawn on the board, after `layout-review.md`, round 1, finding 1.9). Each keep-out is a dashed rectangle on the board's `F.Fab` layer, which the assembly drawing shows and the maker does not build, with the note `DASHED BOXES: NO COAT` beside them; the rectangle's corners are given in board millimetres. The front silkscreen names the board, the three blades, the header's pins and the mains hazard, and is coated over.
- J911, J921 and J941: the blade contact surfaces and the push-on mating area (coat on a blade defeats the push-on contact). Drawn: J941 (23.45, 85.46) to (25.55, 94.63), J921 (31.55, 85.46) to (33.65, 94.63), J911 (42.95, 85.46) to (45.05, 94.63), each the blade's outline grown 0.6 mm; mask the whole blade above the board, and coat the solder pads round its foot.
- J931: the header's pins and the cable connector's mating area. Drawn: (56.48, 56.58) to (64.80, 59.82), the header's outline grown 0.3 mm.
- T911 and T921: the insulated tab faces and the spreader plate's contact area under the compound and the M3 screw (the heat path and the bolted joint stay bare); the leads and pads are coated. Drawn: a thin rectangle along each tab face, which stands in the plate plane y 20.0, 1.0 mm outside the board's top edge: T921 (27.35, 19.45) to (37.85, 20.25), T911 (51.95, 19.45) to (62.45, 20.25).
- U911 and U921: no keep-out; the surface-mount package is soldered to the pads and coated over (**amended 2026-11-19**; the earlier line allowed for sockets, which a surface-mount part does not take).

### Home application steps

As the main board's notes give them (clean with 99.9 % isopropyl alcohol, mask the keep-outs, spray 20 to 25 cm away at 45 degrees in thin coats rotating the board 90 degrees with 3 minutes between coats, 25 to 75 um, cure 24 hours at room temperature before power, inspect under a low-angle light for bare gaps between mains pads). Differences for this board: finish every bring-up repair and fit every hand-fitted part first (a later part is soldered through the film); the plate and triac mounting are done and the heat compound applied before coating, with the tab faces masked; the board's area, a plain rectangle set at layout inside the 102 x 102 mm band (the earlier form's 2422.5 mm2 a face was under 70 cm2), is to be kept under 70 cm2 for both faces together at layout, which the one can the main board uses covers many times over.

Status: criterion C10 is **met on paper**; no coating test is planned and none is claimed.
