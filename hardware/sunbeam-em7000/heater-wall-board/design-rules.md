# Design rules of the heater wall board

Every design rule and net class the heater wall board uses, with its figure, where it is set in KiCad, where the figure comes from, and what stands behind it. The board is two layers at 1 oz finished outer copper, as `manufacturer-limits.yaml` states. The first two rows are the board-wide minimums set before placement, because the placement view takes its resolution from them; the other rows are the rules step's, written before any track is routed.
