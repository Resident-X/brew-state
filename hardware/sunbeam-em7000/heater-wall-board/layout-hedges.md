# Layout hedges: Sunbeam EM7000 heater wall board

One list of what the layout plan carries from the design opinion of 2026-11-18 (`.stage0/wall-design-opinion.md`, read as the design authority for that change). Nothing here is drawable yet: the board outline, footprints and copper do not exist. Each hedge cheaply removes a one-shot risk, and each names its source section or action in that opinion. References are the 9xx series of `schematic-spec.yaml`. Figures marked unsourced are the opinion's engineering knowledge, not read from a document, and are not used as the only support for a verdict.

**Amended 2026-11-18, separation basis.** Hedges 4, 5 and 10 and the pad pairs they touch (the gate-resistor pads of R911, R912, R921 and R922, the TO-220 pin pairs of T911 and T921, the varistor leads, the 2.6 mm gap from mains copper to the wall edge, the triac tab to the earthed plate) and the onsemi opto-triac's 7 mm creepage are accepted by the owner (`owner-sign-off.md` W-2 and W-4), but the signed separation basis names none of them: its heater-switch barrier states no part or exception and none of its entries names a reference of this board. The owner re-declares the basis with those entries before the parts forming the barriers are ordered (`open-items.md`, "What the owner must re-declare in the separation basis before the barrier parts are ordered"); the layout reads each gap against the basis and reports any shortfall as a barrier the basis does not list.

Not hedged, by the opinion's own reasons: a board fuse (every fault but an element-to-OUT short is limited by the element; the entry fuse clears that one through the triac), a gate-MT1 resistor or snubber (resistive load, zero-cross firing, the BW grade's 1000 V/us static dV/dt), a lower-voltage varistor, wide-body or 10 mm opto-triacs, and a bigger triac for heat (section 6, actions A10, A11, A20 to A22).

**Amended 2026-11-19, mounting form.** The wall board's mounting changed on 2026-11-19 (`DEC-HEATER-WALL-BOARD-IS-A-PLAIN-RECTANGLE-SCREWED-TO-A-PRINTED-CASE-ON-A-FLAT-SPREADER`): a flat spreader plate, a plain board retained as the layout chooses (one mounting hole and a printed ledge on 2026-11-19) and a printed case, replacing two ears screwed to a two-leg bracket. Hedges 1 to 12 above hold none of the earlier form's ears, mounts or bracket-leg figures; their wording that named a bracket now says plate (hedges 8, 10, 12), hedge 1 is void as amended (the blades stand on the edge opposite the plate) and hedge 2's 30 mm run limit is dropped; the others stand. The hedges of that form recorded outside this file are **Amended 2026-11-19, void**: the two 16 x 7.5 mm mounting ears and their 3.1 mm holes, the 13.25 mm mains-copper distance from a mounting hole's centre (an earthed-fixing rule from a 3.0 mm extent, the signed 10.0 mm and a 0.25 mm tolerance), the board leg and standing leg of the bracket, and the 57.0 x 42.5 mm outline; the reason is that the plate is flat, the board has no ears, and its holes take screws into plastic. Case design is a later step; nothing here draws the case.

Hedges 7 and 12 stand as layout constraints (a low-voltage part clear of each varistor; no tall part or cable bundle between a triac and its opto-triac, the opto-triacs as far from the triacs and plate as the board allows) and fix no position of those parts: where they sit is the layout agent's.

**Amended 2026-11-19, one hole and no fiducials (layout decision, delegated by the owner).** Hedge 13's count is one: H1, with a printed ledge and fingers or lid ribs retaining the board; hedge 17's exclusion applies to H1 only. The three fiducials FID1 to FID3 are dropped: the four 1206 gate resistors R911, R912, R921 and R922 are hand-fitted (8 joints, excluded from the position file), so no assembled surface-mount part needs a fiducial, and a fiducial-free board is ordered (amended 2026-11-19: the whole board is a bare-PCB order, every part hand-fitted including the two surface-mount opto-triacs, no assembly service). The triacs T911 and T921 take `heater-wall-board:TO-220-3_Vertical_Insulated_OvalPads`. Hedge 16 adds, for the case step, that the plate's lower edge stands at least 6.0 mm above the component face (the triac's leads sit 2.86 mm from the tab face: 2.40 mm from the plate at 5.0 mm, under the 2.6 mm creepage, 3.4 mm at 6.0 mm) and that no case material bridges the two slot mouths on the right edge (`open-items.md`).
