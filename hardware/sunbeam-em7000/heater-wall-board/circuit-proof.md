# Circuit proof: Sunbeam EM7000 heater wall board

The record `docs/board-design-method.md` (section "Proving value-critical circuits before layout") requires before any footprint is placed: one row for every value-critical circuit, with its figure, where the figure comes from, how the circuit is shown to meet it under the worst conditions it will see, and whether it does. This is that record for the heater wall board, for the parts and values of `schematic-spec.yaml`. It follows the form of the main board's `circuit-proof.md`.

## How to read it

- **Met** is `yes` only when the result meets the figure under every condition the row lists. A row is **NO** when its source does not bound a figure the row needs, or when the circuit does not meet the figure; what closes it is written in the Met cell. No figure here was filled in where its source is silent, and no distance or rating was relaxed.
- The arithmetic is `circuit-proof/W_margins.py` with its run `circuit-proof/W_margins.log` (exit status 0; each line is a PASS, FAIL or NOTE). No SPICE deck is committed: every circuit here is a resistive chain or a datasheet limit.
- **Design change of 2026-11-18** (the design opinion of that day, `.stage0/wall-design-opinion.md`, adopted): the triacs are the ST BTA16-800BWRG (C10065, was BTA16-600BRG, C9100) and the opto-triacs the onsemi MOC3083M (C23552, was the Lite-On MOC3083, C10797); the references are the 9xx series. The rows below are re-derived with the new parts' own sheets.
- The onsemi MOC3081M / MOC3082M / MOC3083M sheet (MOC3083M/D Rev. 2, September 2022, `https://www.onsemi.com/pdf/datasheet/moc3083m-d.pdf`) and the ST DS2114 Rev 11 sheet (May 2018, the BTA16 family, the Internet Archive's copy of `https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/bta16.pdf`) were read on 2026-11-18, their pages cited by their own page numbers (onsemi: safety ratings p.2, absolute maximum ratings p.3, characteristics p.4, curves p.5; ST: summary p.1, Tables 1 to 3 p.2, Tables 4 and 5 p.3). The Lite-On sheet no longer governs any figure. The varistor (Zhuhai Hongjiacheng 07D Rev 1.2) figures are those the schematic sheets carry; they were not opened again for this record.
- The supply is the 253 V rms top of the declared span, 357.8 V peak (`separation-basis.yaml` working voltage); the enclosure air is graded at the constraints record's assumed 70 degC (`docs/sunbeam-em7000/controller-board-design-constraints.md`, Enclosure air temperature), an assumption bring-up measures (`DEL-HW-BRING-UP.C13`). This board is not in the controller's air: the owner's working figure for the air at the wall board is 45 degC (stated 2026-10-28, not measured), so 70 degC is the conservative case.
- The board places no controller, no ground net and no resistor on the LED path: the LED series resistor and the sink are on the main board (`main-board/circuit-proof.md`, row "Heater LED drive", and `circuit-proof/D_margins.py` section 5). Row (a) re-derives that stage with the onsemi figures.
- The triac's thermal arithmetic is the assembly judgements' (`docs/sunbeam-em7000/controller-board-division.md`, The heater wall assembly, judged) and is cited, not redone, in row (e).

## Result

Rows (a), (b2), (c) and (d) are met on the records' figures. Row (b) is met on the surge limit, the firing, the latching and the working voltage; its pulse rating is open for want of the 1206 maker's sheet. Row (d) rests on estimates marked unsourced (the surge source impedance, the entry clamp above 50 A, the wall varistor's clamp at currents other than 10 A) and carries one residual risk, a partly failed varistor, that is the owner's to sign with the firmware stuck-on threshold of 2.0 A or less (`open-items.md`, D7). Row (e) and the triac line of the grading table carry the assembly judgements' verdict that the provisional wall cut does not hold continuous full power at 70 degC or at the 100 degC share. The opto-triac's temperature (row f of `W_margins.py`, the grading table) holds at 45 to 50 degC air unless the neighbour rise exceeds 35 to 40 K and is not shown at 70 degC; it is measured at bring-up. Placement may not begin while a row is NO (method, step 4) without the owner's decision recorded; `open-items.md` lists what closes each.
