# Circuit proof: Sunbeam EM7000 heater wall board

The record `docs/board-design-method.md` (section "Proving value-critical circuits before layout") requires before any footprint is placed: one row for every value-critical circuit, with its figure, where the figure comes from, how the circuit is shown to meet it under the worst conditions it will see, and whether it does. This is that record for the heater wall board, for the parts and values of `schematic-spec.yaml`. It follows the form of the main board's `circuit-proof.md`.

## How to read it

- **Met** is `yes` only when the result meets the figure under every condition the row lists. A row is **NO** when its source does not bound a figure the row needs, or when the circuit does not meet the figure; what closes it is written in the Met cell. No figure here was filled in where its source is silent, and no distance or rating was relaxed.
- The arithmetic is `circuit-proof/W_margins.py` with its run `circuit-proof/W_margins.log` (exit status 0; each line is a PASS, FAIL or NOTE). No SPICE deck is committed: every circuit here is a resistive chain or a datasheet limit.
- The Lite-On MOC3083 series sheet (DS70-2001-027 Rev B, 8 numbered pages, the LCSC-hosted copy for C10797, `https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/79a9fbf489164b818fbd18454ca1712e.pdf`) was read on 2026-11-18, its pages cited by the sheet's own page numbers (absolute maximum ratings p.5, characteristics p.6, curves p.7). The ST BTA16-600BRG (Doc ID 7471 Rev 9) and the varistor (Zhuhai Hongjiacheng 07D Rev 1.2) figures are those the schematic sheets carry; they were not opened again for this record.
- The supply is the 253 V rms top of the declared span, 357.8 V peak (`separation-basis.yaml` working voltage); the enclosure air is graded at the constraints record's assumed 70 degC (`docs/sunbeam-em7000/controller-board-design-constraints.md`, Enclosure air temperature), an assumption bring-up measures (`DEL-HW-BRING-UP.C13`). This board is not in the controller's air: the owner's working figure for the air at the wall board is 45 degC (stated 2026-10-28, not measured), so 70 degC is the conservative case.
- The board places no controller, no ground net and no resistor on the LED path: the LED series resistor and the sink are on the main board (`main-board/circuit-proof.md`, row "Heater LED drive", and `circuit-proof/D_margins.py` section 5). Row (a) re-derives that stage with the Lite-On figures.
- The triac's thermal arithmetic is the assembly judgements' (`docs/sunbeam-em7000/controller-board-division.md`, The heater wall assembly, judged) and is cited, not redone, in row (e).

## Result

Rows (a), (b2) and (c) are met. Row (b) is met on the surge limit, the firing and the working voltage; its pulse rating is open for want of the 1206 maker's sheet. Row (d) stays open by the records' own statement (the microsecond withstand and the surge generator's impedance are unsourced) and is the owner's decision D7. Row (e) and the triac line of the grading table carry the assembly judgements' verdict that the provisional wall cut does not hold continuous full power at 70 degC or at the 100 degC share. Placement may not begin while a row is NO (method, step 4) without the owner's decision recorded; `open-items.md` lists what closes each.
