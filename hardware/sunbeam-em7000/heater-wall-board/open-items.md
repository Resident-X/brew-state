Only what the graph, the records and the datasheets could not settle: an owner-held fact, a source that has to be amended, or a choice between workable options. Each item gives the question, the evidence and what settles it. Everything else is in `schematic-spec.yaml`, `circuit-proof.md`, `circuit-verdicts.md`, `bring-up-provision.md`, `fabrication-notes.md` and `constraints-figures.md`. Nothing here is signed or accepted by this file; the owner's decisions are the owner's, and this file records them as held.

## A barrier the separation basis does not list: the 1206 gate-resistor pairs

The signed separation basis (`hardware/sunbeam-em7000/separation-basis.yaml`) lists the 1.8 mm 1206 pad-pair exceptions for the main board's pump gate resistors R704, R705, R714 and R715 (and, by reference, the main board's discharge strings R101 to R104) and for no resistor of this board. This board's gate pairs R101 and R102 (coffee) and R201 and R202 (steam), 200 ohm in 1206 (UNI-ROYAL C18002), are the same footprint and the same circuit (two 200 ohm in series between the opto-triac and the triac gate), and the basis holds those pad pairs at 1.8 mm, a figure it signs for the listed references only. Whether the finished layout's pad gap of these four resistors meets the basis's band figure for the voltage between their pads is the layout check's to read; the pairs are not listed, so a gap short of that figure has no signed exception to stand on. **Reported as a barrier the basis does not list.** The owner re-declares the basis, with exceptions for these four resistors and their bounds, before the part forming the barrier is ordered; no distance is relaxed and the layout is not drawn to a distance that hides it. The exceptions of the basis are keyed by reference designator, and the main board's R101 and R102 are listed there: the wall board's R101 and R102 share those names, so the layout check may judge this board's pairs against the main board's entries or report them as used by no failure; whether it does is read at layout, and a renumbering of this board's gate resistors is the change if it does.

The basis's net-pair entries name the main board's nets (`PUMP_C_GATE_R`, `LINE`, `NEUTRAL` and the like); none names `OUT_C`, `OUT_S`, `RET` or the nine-net Mains set of this board, so every pair of this board's mains nets keeps the full figure for its working voltage, and the pad pairs of one part this board places (the TO-220 triac's three leads on a 2.54 mm pitch, the opto-triac's pins 4 and 6, the varistor's two leads) have no signed entry. The layout leaf meets that by spacing or by slots; any shortfall it cannot close is reported the same way: a barrier the basis does not list, for the owner to re-declare before the part is ordered.

## Sources not found or not read (carried, not filled in)

- **Blade and joiner ratings.** TE Connectivity's drawing C-63849 rev F1 states no current rating for the 63849-1 blades J101, J201 and J401 (class none; the carried 15 A stands against 5.38 A steam, 4.80 A coffee and 10.18 A on the return, `circuit-proof.md` grading table). No maker's document rates the female push-ons or the in-line insulated joiners. Act: ask TE Connectivity for the 63849-1's rated current at 70 degC and a short-time rating; the owner reads or asks for the joiners' rating. A part is not fitted on the carried figure alone without the owner accepting it (division record, The heater wall assembly, judged).
- **Surge withstand.** The triac's and the opto-triac's microsecond withstand, the surge generator's source impedance and energy (`circuit-proof.md` row d): ask STMicroelectronics (BTA16-600BRG) and Lite-On (MOC3083); read the surge immunity test standard named in the constraints record's pending marks.
- **The 1206 resistor's pulse rating.** UNI-ROYAL's maximum overload voltage and pulse rating for the 1206W4F2000T5E (`circuit-proof.md` row b): the maker's datasheet was not opened. Act: read it from the maker, or choose a resistor whose sheet states them.
- **Lite-On sheet against its own table.** IFT is 5 mA maximum at a main-terminal voltage of 3 V (p.6) and Fig. 3 (p.7) shows about 6.7 mA at 20 degC at VP 6 V and RL 100 ohm, above that maximum; the conditions differ and the sheet does not reconcile them. `circuit-proof.md` row (a) holds at either figure (x2.05 and x2.29) and x1.53 with the cold factor added to the Fig. 3 reading. Act: ask Lite-On which figure bounds trigger current. The cold factor 1.5 and the 120 % ageing allowance are the onsemi sheets' and are carried because the Lite-On sheet states neither; the Lite-On sheet states no emitter power derating (the 89.3 mW at 70 degC in `circuit-proof.md` is the 120 mW scaled by Fig. 1's current derating, a derivation).
- **Lite-On VDE approval.** The sheet marks the VDE approval with an asterisk (p.1, "UL / CSA / FIMKO / VDE* approved"); the plain part is not shown to be VDE-approved, and the sheet prints no creepage, clearance or impulse figure. The barrier part's rating against the basis's reinforced target is in `constraints-figures.md`.
- **ST BTA16-600BRG revision.** The sheets cite Doc ID 7471 Rev 9; the division record reads the same family as Rev 11, May 2018. The two are not reconciled and the triac's figures here are the sheets' (`parts-specification.yaml`). The sheet's IT(RMS) against case temperature curve, IL and VGD were not read: the triac's current margin at the case temperatures of the thermal judgement is unsourced.
- **Gate pin absolute maximum.** The BTA16 sheet states no absolute maximum for the gate voltage; `pin-limits.yaml` writes the 600 V of the main terminals so the record has a number, and says so beside the pin.
- **Sheet specification against the drawn Rating fields.** The drawn symbols carry shorter Rating text than `schematic-spec.yaml` states for T101, T201, U101, U201, RV101, RV201 and J301; the parts specification follows the drawn fields (the check compares them) and carries the fuller text in a comment. The spec-generation tool or the drawn fields are to be brought into line by whoever next edits the schematic.
- **Varistor rating shape.** The drawn Rating field leads with "470 V" (the varistor voltage at 1 mA), which the derated-voltage rule reads as the rated maximum; the continuous maximum is 300 VAC / 385 VDC. The stand-off check is the 300 VAC row of `circuit-proof.md` row (d).

## Layout-time and project-file items that no record of this folder can clear

These belong to the KiCad project, the board file and the layout, not to the design records; they are listed so the check's lines for this design are accounted for.

- The project text variable `MAINS` is not set in `heater-wall-board.kicad_pro`; it is set to `present` by the main session (this board carries mains), with the Mains net class for the nine nets `OUT_C`, `OUT_S`, `RET`, `HEAT_C_OPTO_MT`, `HEAT_S_OPTO_MT`, `HEAT_C_GATE_R`, `HEAT_S_GATE_R`, `HEAT_C_TRIAC_G` and `HEAT_S_TRIAC_G`, and a custom rules file with the separation rule; the workmanship text variables and rules, the board-setup minimums of `manufacturer-limits.yaml`, the ignored KiCad checks and the empty board file (outline, footprints) are the layout's.
- The catalogue extract lacks C9100, C10797 and C49072860: JLCPCB's parts library was not read (see `catalogue-extract.yaml`).
- The finished layout's separation, workmanship and neatness checks: nothing is routed.
- The board outline, the mounting ears and holes (3.1 mm at u = -12.5 and 69.5 against the standing leg's ends, resolved by a notch or a mount position keeping 13.25 mm from mains copper) and the bracket drawing are the layout leaf's.
- The copper for 10.18 A: the return and the element-lead copper at 1 oz (`board-rules.yaml`) against the fuse's let-through, which the maker has not stated, and the laminate's temperature limit (constraints record pending mark).

## Revisits recorded and not stretched (enclosure air)

Every part is graded at the constraints record's 70 degC and at the triac's 100 degC share in `circuit-proof.md`. Where no part holds, the revisit is recorded and the rating is not stretched: the triacs at the provisional 80.0 x 30.0 mm window hold the maker's 125 degC at 45 degC with the favourable air coefficient only (116.4 degC) and hold neither 125 degC nor 100 degC at 70 degC (141.4 and 168.8 degC); the least window length that holds 125 degC at 45 degC is 67 mm (favourable) or 110 mm (poor) (division record, Verdict at the provisional size). The revisit is the owner's wall-cut act (D11) and the bring-up measurement of the air and the part temperatures (`DEL-HW-BRING-UP.C13`). The opto-triacs are 30 K under their 100 degC ambient limit at 70 degC. The enclosure air figure is an assumption that bring-up measures.
