## Found for the main board (maintenance, to be authored in the graph)

Not edited here (the main-board folder is outside this change). Round 4 finding 4.2 of the wall board's review found that the main board reads the UNI-ROYAL 1206's maximum working voltage (200 V) and maximum overload voltage (400 V) as the rating of its 200 ohm gate resistors. The correct statement, UNI-ROYAL Thick Film Chip Resistors V.3 (12 Feb 2019) p.5: RCWV = sqrt(rated power x resistance) = sqrt(0.25 W x 200 ohm) = 7.07 V per resistor, capped at the maximum working voltage 200 V; overload voltage 2.5 x RCWV = 17.7 V (capped at 400 V); dielectric withstanding 500 V; no pulse rating stated. The stress on each of R704, R705, R714, R715 is the same arithmetic as this board's (`circuit-proof.md` row (b)): about 10 V per resistor at a firing for tens of microseconds, and up to 178.9 V (162 W) in a false-trigger surge.
