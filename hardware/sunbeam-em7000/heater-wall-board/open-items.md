# Open items: Sunbeam EM7000 heater wall board, design records

Only what the graph, the records and the datasheets could not settle: an owner-held fact, a source that has to be amended, or a choice between workable options. Each item gives the question, the evidence and what settles it. Everything else is in `schematic-spec.yaml`, `circuit-proof.md`, `circuit-verdicts.md`, `bring-up-provision.md`, `fabrication-notes.md` and `constraints-figures.md`. Nothing here is signed or accepted by this file; the owner's decisions are the owner's, and this file records them as held.

**Requirement to carry to the graph (firmware and sensing; the main session authors the node).** The stuck-on current threshold of the supply-current check (the check that reads a heater's current while it is commanded off) must be set so that it trips at 2.0 A or less, so that the 2.7 A rms of a partly failed wall varistor fed through a 47 ohm element (253^2 / (4 x 47) = 340 W, `W_margins.py` section d) is seen. The division record allows the threshold anywhere in 0.78 to 4.10 A, which is not enough. Source: the design opinion of 2026-11-18, section 1, "What happens if the varistor fails", and action table A3; arithmetic `circuit-proof/W_margins.log`. No firmware parameter was changed by this record: `firmware/params` is untouched, and whether an existing parameter already sits at or under 2.0 A is for the main session to read.

## A barrier the separation basis does not list: the 1206 gate-resistor pairs and the part pairs

The signed separation basis (`hardware/sunbeam-em7000/separation-basis.yaml`) lists the 1.8 mm 1206 pad-pair exceptions for the main board's pump gate resistors R704, R705, R714 and R715 (and, by reference, the main board's discharge strings R101 to R104) and for no resistor of this board. This board's gate pairs R911 and R912 (coffee) and R921 and R922 (steam), 200 ohm in 1206 (UNI-ROYAL C18002), are the same footprint and the same circuit (two 200 ohm in series between the opto-triac and the triac gate), and the basis holds those pad pairs at 1.8 mm, a figure it signs for the listed references only. Whether the finished layout's pad gap of these four resistors meets the basis's band figure for the voltage between their pads is the layout check's to read; the pairs are not listed, so a gap short of that figure has no signed exception to stand on. **Reported as a barrier the basis does not list.** The owner re-declares the basis, with exceptions for these four resistors and their bounds, before the part forming the barrier is ordered; no distance is relaxed and the layout is not drawn to a distance that hides it. **The reference collision is closed by the design change of 2026-11-18**: the exceptions of the basis are keyed by reference designator and the main board's R101 and R102 are listed there, so this board's references moved to the 9xx series (T911, U911, R911, R912, RV911, J911; T921, U921, R921, R922, RV921, J921; J931; J941); none equals a reference of the main board or of the basis (checked by searching the main-board folder, the basis and the controller part record for each of the fourteen).

The basis's net-pair entries name the main board's nets (`PUMP_C_GATE_R`, `LINE`, `NEUTRAL` and the like); none names `OUT_C`, `OUT_S`, `RET` or the nine-net Mains set of this board, so every pair of this board's mains nets keeps the full figure for its working voltage, and the pad pairs of one part this board places (the TO-220 triac's three leads on a 2.54 mm pitch, the opto-triac's pins 4 and 6, the varistor's two leads) have no signed entry. The layout leaf meets that by spacing or by slots (`layout-hedges.md`: oval triac pads for a pin gap of at least 1.0 mm); any shortfall it cannot close is reported the same way: a barrier the basis does not list, for the owner to re-declare before the part is ordered.

## Sources not found or not read (carried, not filled in)

- **Blade and joiner ratings.** TE Connectivity's drawing C-63849 rev F1 states no current rating for the 63849-1 blades J911, J921 and J941 (class none; the carried 15 A stands against 5.38 A steam, 4.80 A coffee and 10.18 A on the return, `circuit-proof.md` grading table). No maker's document rates the female push-ons or the in-line insulated joiners. Act: ask TE Connectivity for the 63849-1's rated current at 70 degC and a short-time rating; the owner reads or asks for the joiners' rating. A part is not fitted on the carried figure alone without the owner accepting it (division record, The heater wall assembly, judged).
- **Surge withstand, unsourced inputs.** The surge generator's source impedance (2 ohm, IEC 61000-4-5 not read) and the 1 kV household-appliance level (CISPR 14-2 not read), the main board's entry clamp above 50 A (the 14D curve not read), the 07D471K's clamp at currents other than the 10 A point (its V-I curve could not be fetched), the opto-triac's turn-on time, and the triac's and opto-triac's microsecond withstand: `circuit-proof.md` row (d) marks each use. None reverses the verdict (the 800 V triac holds for any entry clamp up to about 1.2 kV). Act: read the standards; ask ST and onsemi for a microsecond withstand if wanted.
- **The 1206 resistor's pulse rating.** UNI-ROYAL's maximum overload voltage and pulse rating for the 1206W4F2000T5E (`circuit-proof.md` row b): the maker's datasheet was not opened. Act: read it from the maker, or choose a resistor whose sheet states them.
- **Closed by the design change of 2026-11-18.** The Lite-On sheet's disagreement with its own table (IFT against its Fig. 3), its missing emitter power derating, its VDE asterisk and its silence on creepage, clearance and impulse all went with the part: the onsemi MOC3083M sheet states creepage and clearance at least 7 mm, VIOTM 6000 Vpk, VIORM 850 Vpk and the emitter power derating, and its drive arithmetic is the main board's own (x1.99). The ST revision item also closes: the triac's figures come from DS2114 Rev 11 (May 2018) for the BW 800 V grade; the sheet's revision history lists no electrical change between Rev 9 (Dec 2009) and Rev 11 other than the product status and summary and the T1650 package, and the B-grade figures the records carried under Doc ID 7471 Rev 9 (IGT 50 mA, VGT 1.3 V, IH 50 mA, dV/dt 400 V/us, (dV/dt)c 10 V/us, Rth(j-c) 2.1 K/W, ITSM 160 A, VDSM VDRM + 100 V) agree with Rev 11's Tables 1 to 5; Rev 9 itself was not opened again. The earlier table citations (Table 4 for the B column, Table 6 for Rth) were Rev 11's Tables 3 and 5.
- **Gate pin absolute maximum, fixed.** The BTA16 sheet states no gate voltage maximum, only IGM 4 A (20 us, Tj 125 degC) and PG(AV) 1 W (Table 1 p.2); the earlier 600 V gate entry in `pin-limits.yaml` was the main terminals' voltage and is removed. - **Sheet specification against the drawn Rating fields.** The drawn symbols carry shorter Rating text than `schematic-spec.yaml` states for T911, T921, U911, U921, RV911, RV921 and J931; the parts specification follows the drawn fields (the check compares them) and carries the fuller text in a comment. ## Revisits recorded and not stretched (enclosure air)

Every part is graded at the constraints record's 70 degC and at the triac's 100 degC share in `circuit-proof.md`. Where no part holds, the revisit is recorded and the rating is not stretched: the triacs at the provisional 80.0 x 30.0 mm window hold the maker's 125 degC at 45 degC with the favourable air coefficient only (116.4 degC) and hold neither 125 degC nor 100 degC at 70 degC (141.4 and 168.8 degC); the least window length that holds 125 degC at 45 degC is 67 mm (favourable) or 110 mm (poor) (division record, Verdict at the provisional size). The revisit is the owner's wall-cut act (D11) and the bring-up measurement of the air and the part temperatures, and the bracket-to-protective-earth resistance (the tab barrier relies on it; a toothed washer under one wall bolt) (`DEL-HW-BRING-UP.C13`). The opto-triacs (onsemi, 85 degC ambient limit) have 15 K of headroom at 70 degC and 35 to 40 K at the owner's 45 to 50 degC for the rise from the triac leg 20.0 mm away, which is unmeasured; 70 degC air does not show them inside the 88 degC junction share (`circuit-proof.md`, grading table). The enclosure air figure is an assumption that bring-up measures.

## Layout hedges

For the layout plan; none is drawable until the board is laid out. Sources and reasons are in `layout-hedges.md` (the design opinion of 2026-11-18, sections 1, 2, 4 and 5).

- Return blade J941 between the two triacs; RET and element-lead copper on both layers, 5 mm or wider on each, blade and triac pads connected solid (no thermal relief).
- Oval triac pads so the gap between pins is at least 1.0 mm (the three pin pairs listed under D15).
- A slot 1.5 mm wide under each opto-triac, open at the edge (the division record has 1.0 mm); no copper between the opto rows but the pads; varistor bodies at least about 5 mm from low-voltage parts.
- Fitting and bring-up: a toothed washer on one wall bolt and the earth bond measured at bring-up; the board supported when the blades are fitted.
