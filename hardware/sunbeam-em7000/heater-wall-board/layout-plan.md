# Layout plan of the Sunbeam EM7000 heater wall board

This is the floorplan and the plan for the heater wall board, written before any footprint is placed, as `docs/board-design-method.md` section "Planning the layout" asks. The board is two copper layers (F.Cu and B.Cu), 1 oz finished outer copper, 1.6 mm, with every part on the front face (the four 1206 gate resistors and the opto-triacs U911 and U921 are the surface-mount parts, all hand-fitted; every other part is through-hole) and nothing on the back face. It is drawn from the schematic in this folder, the records `layout-hedges.md`, `owner-sign-off.md`, `separation-basis.yaml`, `board-rules.yaml`, `pin-allocation.yaml`, `fabrication-notes.md` and `circuit-proof.md`, and the owner's direction of 2026-11-19. The schematic's netlist holds 15 parts: the blades J911, J921 and J941, the header J931, the triacs T911 and T921, the opto-triacs U911 and U921, the gate resistors R911, R912, R921 and R922, the varistors RV911 and RV921, and the mounting hole H1.

**Coordinates.** Board coordinates are the board editor's: x to the right, y down, in millimetres. The plane y = 20.0 is the inner face of the flat earthed aluminium plate that carries the two triacs: the tab faces of T911 and T921 lie in it. The board's top edge stands 1.0 mm behind it, at y = 21.0, and the case's fixed tray carries an insulating wall in that gap against the plate. The plate stands along the top edge with its lower edge at least 6.0 mm above the component face. The blades stand on the bottom edge (y = 96.0), opposite the plate. The low-voltage parts stand on the right edge at mid depth. The triac pad row is at y 22.86; each triac pad's copper is offset 0.40 mm away from the tab (y 22.01 to 24.51), its hole stays at y 22.86.

**Outline.** The outline is a plain rectangle from (20.0, 21.0) to (65.0, 96.0), 45.0 mm by 75.0 mm, with two 1.5 mm slots open at the right edge (slot A from (51.0, 49.44) to (65.0, 50.94), slot B from (51.0, 65.46) to (65.0, 66.96)). There is one mounting hole, H1, at (61.50, 92.50) in the bottom-right pocket, and no Mains copper lies left of x 21.5, leaving a 1.5 mm copper-free border on the left and bottom edges for the case's ledge. The plan rests on four assumptions the owner is asked to sign: A1, the plate's inner-face plane is y 20.0, 1.0 mm in front of the board's top edge, and the plate's lower edge stands at least 6.0 mm above the component face; A2, the distance from a triac's tab face to its lead centre is 2.86 mm nominal (ST DS2114 Rev 11, Table 6 and Figure 13: 2.65 to 3.07 mm); A3, T911 and T921 use a board-library footprint with oval pads and a corrected body and courtyard, pinout unchanged; A4, the blades stand with their wide face along the board's depth, 8.1 mm (between J941 and J921) and 11.4 mm (between J921 and J911) apart, and the insulated push-on receptacles are about 6 mm thick.

**Design choices a senior engineer would flag, one line each.**

- Plate along the top edge, blades on the bottom edge, low voltage on the right edge: the gate side of both triacs is their right-hand pin, so the right edge is the natural side for the opto-triacs and the header, and the three heavy runs go straight from the triacs to the blades.
- The three heavy nets run as parallel strips in the order RET, OUT_S, OUT_C from left to right, RET 6.0 mm and OUT_S, OUT_C 5.0 mm wide on both layers, with the single-layer stretches BOARD-4 lists.
- One crossing of heavy nets is unavoidable on any outline. The pin order along the top edge is RET, OUT_S, gate, RET, OUT_C, gate, with the pads 1.3 mm from the edge; the two RET pins can be joined only below the pads, and OUT_S, whose blade is on the bottom edge, lies between them, so RET and OUT_S cross once. Here RET from T911 pin 1 runs on B.Cu only, under OUT_S and the steam gate net, which stay on F.Cu there (BOARD-5).
- Rejected: a RET bar behind the triacs under the plate (10 A of mains copper 5 mm from the earthed plate, and a harder case); the OUT_S blade set 8 mm in from the bottom edge with RET passing below it (a fourth heavy column about 8 mm wider, the blade off the edge); RET in the centre (the steam thin nets would then cross the merged 10.18 A RET); low voltage in the bottom centre (about 56 mm wide before holes); the order RET, OUT_C, OUT_S (two heavy lateral runs and no place for RV921).
- The two opto-triacs stand stacked on the right edge with horizontal pin rows and a 1.5 mm slot open at the right edge between the rows of each; U911 (coffee) is upper with its mains row on top and U921 (steam) lower with its mains row at the bottom, so the two low-voltage rows face each other with J931 between them.
- The opto-triac U911 stands 22.41 mm and U921 38.44 mm from the nearest triac body, body to body; the courtyards are 19.59 mm and 35.62 mm away and U911's nearest pad 19.82 mm, because the surface-mount pads reach further out than the DIP's, and BOARD-8 passes on the body distance and goes on the owner's list; the varistors stand away from the low-voltage parts, as `layout-hedges.md` 7, 8 and 12 ask.
- The board has one via, a low-voltage one on +5V_WB; no Mains net has a via, because the schematic swapped the opto-triacs' main-terminal pins (decision D4 below, reversed by the schematic on 2026-11-19).

## Decisions taken under the owner's delegation of 2026-11-19 (not part of the plan's tables)

The owner delegated the open questions to the layout agent; these are its decisions. A wider form (61.0 mm wide, four mounting holes and a left strip) was rejected in favour of the 45.0 mm board.

- D1, form: the outline is (20.0, 21.0) to (65.0, 96.0), 45.0 mm by 75.0 mm, with one mounting hole, H1, at (61.50, 92.50). The owner's main unknown is the width along the plate, 45 mm against 61 mm. The schematic deletes the other three holes.
- D2, fiducials: there are none. The four 1206 gate resistors R911, R912, R921 and R922 are hand-fitted (eight joints, `hand: true` to be recorded in `schematic-spec.yaml`) and excluded from the position file, so no surface-mount part is machine-placed and MFG-10 does not apply. - D3, plate height: the case step holds the plate's lower edge at least 6.0 mm above the board's component face.
