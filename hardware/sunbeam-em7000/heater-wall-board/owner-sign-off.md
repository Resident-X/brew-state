# Owner sign-off list: Sunbeam EM7000 heater wall board

This is the record of the project owner's acceptances for the heater wall board, written 2026-11-18 after schematic review round 2 (`schematic-review.md`, Findings 2.1 to 2.11 and "Round 1 findings, closure") found the owner's decisions of that day entered in no file. It follows the form of the main board's `main-board/owner-sign-off.md`: one entry per decision, each saying who signed, on what day, which finding it closes, and on what ground. Each entry carries `accepted_by` and `accepted_on`, the pair the method's step 5 asks of a safety finding closed by a recorded reason.

**Two kinds of entry, marked in each.** "The owner's own words" means David Baker, the project owner, accepted it himself on 2026-11-18 in the working session, after the risks were put to him in plain terms: no shock risk in normal use; the residuals are fault behaviour inside an enclosure, contained by the interlock relay K201, the fuse, the firmware's stuck-on current threshold, the earth bond measured at bring-up, and an RCD-protected outlet with the lamp current limiter at first power. His words, the only words of his this record quotes: "I'm happy with these and sign as accepted". They cover W-1 to W-4. On 2026-11-19 he accepted W-5c in his words (below) and replied "Accept 1 2 and 3" to three items put to him in plain terms (W-6 the heater-chain independence statement, the gate-resistor residual, the LED return shorted on the cable); those words cover W-6 and W-7 and are recorded in each; his "Yes" to the two carry questions of the same day is W-8. "Signed on behalf" means the design owner decided it and signed it on the project owner's behalf under his standing instruction of 2026-11-07 (sign owner items on his behalf and list them at the end of the work; stop only for a real safety or performance impact); no word of his is quoted for those entries. They are W-5a, W-5b and W-5d.

**This list does not change the separation basis.** `hardware/sunbeam-em7000/separation-basis.yaml` is a signed record and is not edited here. Its heater-switch barrier names no part, rating or exception for the onsemi opto-triac, the triac tab pair or this board's pad pairs, and its 42 exception entries name no reference of this board. The acceptances W-2 and W-4 are the owner's, but the basis does not yet carry them: the entries to add are listed in `open-items.md`, "What the owner must re-declare in the separation basis before the barrier parts are ordered".

## Look at these first

- W-3: a partly failed varistor fed through the element conducts about 2.7 A (340.5 W in the varistor alone and about the same again in the element, 681 W together) and no fuse clears it. The firmware's stuck-on current threshold, with every heater commanded off, must trip at 2.0 A or less. That is required by `REQ-ACTUATION-CONFIRMATION-001.C10` (graph, commit 1977f86c); no firmware parameter was changed and no firmware delivers it yet.
- W-2 and W-4: the basis does not carry them yet; the owner re-declares it before the order.
- W-6 and W-7 (accepted by David in his words on 2026-11-19, "Accept 1 2 and 3"): a heater stuck on is bounded only by the element's thermostats and, if the controller stops refreshing or the 5 V rail collapses, by K201; an LED return shorted on the cable bypasses the 180 ohm and can run the LED over its 60 mA maximum below 54 ohm; the gate resistors have no pulse rating in their sheet.
- W-5c: the owner accepted finding 2.7 in his words on 2026-11-19, with a fact he was given: the triac's current share at its case temperature is short of the steam element's current under either reading of ST's curve. W-5a, W-5b and W-5d are signed on behalf (last section).
- "Carried to named later steps" (below) is the one table of what each carried finding waits on, the criterion that asks for it and who accepted it.
