It follows the form of the main board's `main-board/owner-sign-off.md`: one entry per decision, each saying who signed, on what day, which finding it closes, and on what ground. Each entry carries `accepted_by` and `accepted_on`, the pair the method's step 5 asks of a safety finding closed by a recorded reason.

**The record is sectioned by who accepted.** There are 19 entries, W-1 to W-16 with W-5 split into W-5a, W-5b, W-5c and W-5d. 16 are David Baker's own acceptance, in his words, in the section "Owner's own words" (subsections by the day he said them: 2026-11-18: W-1, W-2, W-3, W-4; 2026-11-19: W-5c, W-6, W-7, W-8, W-9, W-10, W-11, W-12; 2026-11-19 and 2026-11-20: W-13; 2026-11-20: W-14, W-15, W-16). 3 are signed on his behalf, in the section "Signed on behalf".
