Criterion C8 (every part graded against the enclosure air) is the grading table of `circuit-proof.md`, "Grading every part against the enclosure air", at 70 degC and at the triac's 100 degC share. This file does not edit the constraints record; where a mark ends here, the constraints record is brought into line by the work that owns it.

## Source of the shares carried

The temperature share 0.8 (0.8 x 125 = 100 degC) and the current share 0.5 (8 A for the triac) are the assembly judgements' stated reasons, not read from any document, carried unchanged into `derating-policy.yaml` as the leaf requires. The connector and joiner figure, 15 A, is carried against a maker rating once one is read; none is.
