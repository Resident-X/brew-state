# Design rules of the layout review proof design

Every design rule and net class `review-proof` uses, with its figure, where it is set in KiCad, where the figure comes from, and what stands behind it. The board is four layers at 2 oz finished copper, made by JLCPCB, as its constraints record `constraints.md` states. The manufacturer's limits are read from JLCPCB's rigid PCB capabilities page, https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-capabilities, and its PCB Assembly FAQ (Part 2), https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/pcb-assembly-faqs-part-2, both read on 2026-10-13. Each row quotes the figure as the page gives it. The confirmation column quotes `task hw:rules-confirm -- --board review-proof.kicad_pcb --project review-proof.kicad_pro --class Mains --class Power --class Supply --class Ground --class Sensing`, which ended `RESULT | CONFIRMED | KiCad loads review-proof.kicad_dru and places nets in each of Mains, Power, Supply, Ground, Sensing`.
