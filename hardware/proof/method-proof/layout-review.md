### Plan and free pass

**Regions.** The floorplan's two regions and their parts are what the layout holds. The low voltage region is (100, 100) to (119, 130) and the digital region (119, 100) to (140, 130); the board carries one rule area for each, on F.Cu, B.Cu, In1.Cu and In2.Cu, at exactly those bounds (every layer view draws them), and none for the mains and analog sensing regions, which the plan records as none. `measures.txt` lists the same parts per region as the plan, with OUTSIDE 0, and the courtyards read off the fabrication outlines lie inside their regions, JP2's to x 118.77 and C3's from x 119.22 being the nearest to the line. `layout-plan.md` holds no board's own items (one row, none), so there is nothing further to judge there.

**Connectors.** The edges are met: J1 and J2 stand at the left edge (bodies from x 100.6) and J3 at the right edge (body to x 138.3, 1.7 mm inside the outline); JP1 and JP2 are link positions with no edge, as the table says.
