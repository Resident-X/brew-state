## Row 1: the arithmetic

The LED, R1 and PA0 are in series from `+5V` to ground: I = (Vs − VF − VOL) / R1, with Vs the voltage on `+5V`, VF the LED's forward voltage and VOL the voltage on PA0 while it sinks I.

- **Vs** is 4.75 V to 5.25 V, the constraints record's supply input voltage.
- **R1** is 1.2 kΩ, read from the netlist. Its tolerance is ±1 % and its temperature coefficient ±100 ppm/K, Vishay data sheet 20035 (CRCW0603, revision 14-Apr-2026), *Features and TCR table*, the 1 % type at 100 ppm/K. The datasheet states no reference temperature for the coefficient, so the temperature difference is taken as the whole of its operating range, -55 °C to +155 °C, 210 K, which needs no reference: R1 is between 1163.1 Ω and 1237.5 Ω.
- **VOL** is at most 0.45 V, TI data sheet § 7.10.1, *Digital IO, Electrical Characteristics*, the ODIO row "Low level output voltage", IOL,max = 8 mA, VDD ≥ 2.7 V, -40 °C to 125 °C. The current here never exceeds 4.514 mA, below the 8 mA the figure is stated for, and a sinking pin's output voltage does not rise as its current falls. Its lower bound is 0 V.
- **VF** is at most 2.4 V at 20 mA and 25 °C, Vishay data sheet 82437 (revision 1.6, 30-Mar-2023), *Optical and Electrical Characteristics*, VLMG1300 yellow green, with the datasheet's note that the forward voltage is measured to ±0.1 V: 2.5 V. At the 1 mA to 5 mA this circuit carries, the forward voltage is no higher than at 20 mA. Its lower bound is 0 V: the datasheet gives no minimum, and none at a current this low.
- **Maximum current**: the supply at its highest, 5.25 V, over the lowest R1, 1163.1 Ω, with VF and VOL at their lower bound of 0 V: 4.514 mA, below 5 mA, below the 8 mA of the VOL figure and below the LED's 30 mA DC forward current, Vishay data sheet 82437, *Absolute Maximum Ratings*.
- **Minimum current**: the supply at its lowest, 4.75 V, less VF 2.5 V and VOL 0.45 V, over the highest R1, 1237.5 Ω: 1.455 mA, above 1 mA. The largest forward voltage the 1 mA minimum tolerates is 3.063 V.
- **R1's dissipation** at the maximum current and the highest R1 is 25.2 mW, below the 100 mW rated dissipation of the 0603 size, P70, Vishay data sheet 20035, *Features*.

VOL's condition VDD ≥ 2.7 V is met by row 4: VDD is at least 3.120 V.

## Rows 2 to 5: the inputs

- **C1** is a KEMET T520B476M010ATE035, 47 µF, tolerance 20 %, 10 VDC (105 °C); **C2** a KEMET T520B157M006ATE025, 150 µF, tolerance 20 %, 6.3 VDC (105 °C), ESR 25 mΩ (100 kHz, 25 °C), dissipation factor 8 % (120 Hz, 25 °C); each from its part specification sheet from KEMET's own site, `https://search.kemet.com/download/specsheet/<part number>`, generated 10/16/2026. Polymer tantalum is used because the Microchip datasheet bounds the output capacitor's ESR at 2.0 Ω and no ceramic capacitor datasheet read bounds one: the X7R datasheet's dissipation factor for 4.7 µF at 16 V, 0805, is 10 % at 1 kHz, KEMET C1002_X7R (YAGEO Group, 2026-09-01), *Performance and Reliability*, which bounds a 4.7 µF part's ESR at 1 kHz at 3.8 Ω.
- C2's ESR is bounded from its dissipation factor: ESR ≤ DF / (2π f C), with DF 8 %, f 120 Hz and C the lowest capacitance 120 µF: 0.884 Ω, below 2.0 Ω. The sheet's 25 mΩ is not labelled a maximum, so it is not used as a bound.
- **C3** is a KEMET C0805C106K8RACTU, 10 µF, K = ±10 %, rated voltage code 8 = 10 V, X7R, KEMET C1002_X7R data sheet (YAGEO Group, 2026-09-01), *Ordering Information*, https://content.kemet.com/datasheets/KEM_C1002_X7R_SMD.pdf.
- **The 3.3 V rail** is U1's output, 3.3 V × (1 ± (3.0 % + 0.95 % + 1.5 %)): 3.120 V to 3.480 V. The 0.95 % is the line regulation, 1.0 %/V, times the supply span's highest distance from VR + 1 V = 4.3 V, 0.95 V. U1 is the Microchip MCP1700T-3302E/TT, 3.3 V, § *Product Identification System*.

## Not bounded by a source

These are the conditions the figure's range implies that no datasheet read states as a limit. None is covered by a figure; each is shown only by the margin the result has.

1. **The LED's forward voltage over 0 °C to 50 °C.** Vishay data sheet 82437 states it at 25 °C only. The minimum current tolerates a forward voltage up to 3.063 V, 0.563 V above the datasheet's 2.5 V.
2. **The voltage lost between J1 pin 1 and `+5V`**, through the isolating link JP1 and the copper, which no datasheet read bounds as a limit: the Samtec data sheets give the link's current rating, 4.3 A per pin, and no contact resistance. At the same 0.563 V of margin it tolerates a resistance of 1.1 Ω at 0.5 A.
3. **C2's and C1's capacitance and C2's ESR over 0 °C to 50 °C**; the part sheets state them at 25 °C. C2's minimum 120 µF is 120 times the 1.0 µF needed, and its ESR bound 0.884 Ω has 2.26 times margin.
4. **A polymer capacitor's DC bias**: the sheets state no dependence of capacitance on the applied voltage.
5. **"Low-ESR" for C3**: the TI datasheet gives no figure for it.

## Circuits considered and not listed

- **No sensing front end, no zero-crossing detector**: the schematic carries neither.
- **No conductor relies on a protective device**: the schematic carries no fuse and the constraints record declares none and no fault current, so no conductor row exists. The isolating links JP1 and JP2 are rated for the 0.5 A continuous current by Samtec's SNT shunt datasheet, *Specifications*, 4.3 A per pin; that is checked in `bring-up-provision.md`.
- **U1's dissipation**: it depends on no component value on the board, and the Microchip datasheet gives the SOT-23 junction-to-ambient thermal resistance, 212 °C/W, as a typical value only, so no junction temperature can be sourced for it.
- **C4 and C5**, 100 nF output filter capacitors at J2's pins 2 and 1: no datasheet read states a requirement of their value, and neither is on a circuit that works or fails by it.
- **The PA1 header J3** carries a signal out of the board and holds no value that decides whether it works.
